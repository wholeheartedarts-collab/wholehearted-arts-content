# Brand Voice

## If writing samples exist

Analyze provided posts, articles, book excerpts, or newsletters for:

- Vocabulary and sentence structure
- Rhythm and paragraph length
- Point of view and level of formality
- Storytelling patterns and how examples/evidence are used
- Preferred hooks and opening patterns
- Calls to action
- Punctuation habits (e.g., em dash usage — only keep them if the client's
  real samples actually use them)
- Language or phrasing to avoid

Save/update: `clients/[client-slug]/profile/brand-voice.md`. Remove any
PROVISIONAL flag once real samples back the guide.

## If no samples exist yet (current state for wholehearted-arts)

Build a provisional guide from the client profile's stated voice
description plus platform best-practice research (see [[research]] for
sourcing method — use the cheapest capable model). For Wholehearted Arts
specifically, the starting voice signal is: **Christian, artist, energetic,
creative** — warm and personally expressive, not corporate thought-leader
voice. Two audiences (art collectors vs. automation-buying businesses)
may call for slightly different registers within the same core voice —
note this as an open question rather than flattening it.

Mark the file PROVISIONAL at the top and set a review trigger: **re-run
this analysis after the first 3 published posts**, using those posts plus
any human feedback as the new samples.

## Quality rules (apply regardless of source)

- Avoid generic AI phrasing, empty hype, fake intimacy, and unsupported
  superlatives ("game-changing," "unlock," "in today's fast-paced world").
- Avoid repetitive sentence patterns and formulaic hook structures.
- Never fabricate a personal experience, result, or client story attributed
  to the human — see [[factual-verification]].
- Demonstrate expertise by showing (examples, specifics, real work) rather
  than telling (claims of expertise).
- Preserve the client's real judgment and voice over "what performs well"
  when the two conflict — flag the tension rather than silently choosing.

## Output format

`clients/[client-slug]/profile/brand-voice.md` should be usable directly
by [[linkedin-writing]]: a short description plus a few concrete
before/after or good/bad example lines, not just abstract adjectives.
