# Instagram Writing

## Prerequisites

- `research.md` for the topic must exist (see [[research]]).
- `clients/[client-slug]/profile/brand-voice.md` must exist (see
  [[brand-voice]]) — provisional is acceptable, missing is not.
- A final image (or a clear plan for one) should exist or be imminent —
  Instagram is image-led; the caption supports the image, not the other
  way around. Don't write the caption in a vacuum from the topic alone.

## Instagram-specific requirements

- **Opening**: the first ~125 characters must work standalone — that's
  roughly where Instagram truncates before "... more." Shorter than
  LinkedIn's ~210-character cutoff — do not reuse a LinkedIn hook as-is.
- **Structure**: short lines, line breaks between thoughts are native and
  expected. Emoji as light punctuation/visual breaks are native to the
  platform — use them only if they fit Agata's actual voice (check
  brand-voice.md), not as a default.
- **Body**: the caption's job is to add context, story, or meaning to the
  image — not to carry the whole message alone the way a LinkedIn post
  does. Assume the viewer already saw the image before reading a word of
  the caption.
- **Links**: Instagram captions do not support clickable links. Never
  write a raw URL expecting it to be clickable. Use "link in bio" or
  point to a Story/Highlight if a link is genuinely needed — otherwise
  skip it.
- **Length**: shorter than a typical LinkedIn post. Favor a length that
  earns the "more" tap without over-explaining — Instagram audiences
  scan fast.
- **CTA**: natural, matched to the client's approved CTAs (see profile).
  Default soft CTA if none approved — invite comments, saves, or DMs.
- **Hashtags**: maximum 5 tags (Agata's explicit rule, 2026-08-09 —
  overrides general Instagram-platform norms). This is intentionally
  different from LinkedIn's "small handful" rule; do not copy that
  number over, and do not default to a higher count even though wider
  ranges are common Instagram practice generally. Mix broad art/faith-
  community tags with specific ones (e.g., piece title, medium,
  "#mixedmediaart") within that cap of 5. Never use tags unrelated to
  the actual post just to chase reach.
- **Account type**: this client posts from a **Business/Creator account**
  for WholeheartedArts specifically (confirmed by Agata) — this is a
  brand/artist presence, not a strictly personal profile, but the voice
  should still read as Agata herself, warm and personal, not corporate.

## Two-audience awareness

Same split as [[linkedin-writing]]: art collectors vs. automation-buying
businesses. Instagram skews more toward the **art collectors** audience
by nature of the platform (visual-first) — don't force AI-automation
content onto Instagram just for cross-posting symmetry if it doesn't fit
the format. It's fine for a topic to be LinkedIn+Instagram only, or
Instagram-only, if that's genuinely the better fit — flag this rather
than silently forcing all three platforms every time.

## Output

Save `clients/[client-slug]/posts/[NNN-topic-slug]/instagram-post.md`:

```
## Hook
[opening ~125 characters, must stand alone]

## Caption body
[rest of the caption]

## CTA
[final line(s)]

## Hashtags
[5-15 tags, with brief rationale for the mix]

## Angle used
[which research angle this draft took, and which audience/pillar it serves]
```

## Hard rules

- Every factual claim must trace to `research.md` — no invented stats,
  quotes, or stories. See [[factual-verification]].
- No politics (client's explicit avoid-list item).
- Avoid AI-slop phrasing and generic IG-hype ("swipe up for more!",
  excessive exclamation points) — see [[brand-voice]] quality rules.
- Do not generate or reference image content here — imagery happens in
  [[image-generation]], sized for Instagram per [[visual-style]].
- Never include a clickable-link expectation in the caption text.
