## Hook
Some pieces come with a whole story behind them. This one came with just a title — and the title said enough.

## Body
"Breaking the Chains." Acrylic on canvas, 18x24.

I don't have a long story to tell you about this one — no specific verse I was holding in my mind while I painted it, no particular morning it came from. Just those three words, and the shapes that showed up on the canvas when I sat with them.

I think that's its own kind of honest. Not every piece of art — or every season of a person's life — comes with a tidy explanation. Sometimes you just know something in you is tired of being held, and you make the mark anyway.

The phrase itself is old. It shows up all through scripture — the psalmist writing about being brought "out of darkness... and broke away their chains" (Psalm 107:14), Jesus telling people that real freedom isn't the surface kind: "if the Son sets you free, you will be free indeed" (John 8:36). I didn't set out to illustrate either verse here. But I don't think it's an accident that the words found their way onto this canvas anyway.
