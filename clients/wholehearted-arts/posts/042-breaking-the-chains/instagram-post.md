## Caption body
"Breaking the Chains."
Acrylic on canvas, 18x24.

No specific verse I had in mind while I painted it. No particular morning it came from.

Just those three words, and the shapes that showed up on the canvas when I sat with them.

Maybe that's its own kind of honest — not everything comes with a tidy explanation. Sometimes you just know something in you is tired of being held. And you make the mark anyway.
