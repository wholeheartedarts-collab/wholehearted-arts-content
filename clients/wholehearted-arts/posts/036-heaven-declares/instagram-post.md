## Hook
The figure in this piece isn't made of paint.

It's made of torn Scripture, raised up — rough edges and all.

## Caption body
I left the paper and fabric rough on purpose.

"Heaven Declares" came out of Psalm 19 — not just the opening line about the heavens, but the part where it turns to God's own Word: "sweeter than honey, than honey from the honeycomb."

We're created beautifully, and still marked by weakness and process. Perfectly imperfect.

So the figure here is lifted, not hidden. What's fragmented gets offered. What's unfinished gets lifted. And somewhere in that offering, the heart gets made whole.

"We have this treasure in jars of clay" (2 Corinthians 4:7) — that's the whole piece, really.

40" x 30", fabric, paper, and acrylic, in a silver frame.
