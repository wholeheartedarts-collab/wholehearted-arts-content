## Hook
"A cradle of support waiting to embrace us" — even in the trials. That's what I wanted this piece to say.

## Caption body
Meet "In the Cradle of Faith" — mixed media, acrylic and fiber, 20"x20".

I built this one out of layers. Vibrant color, texture you can almost feel, forms that don't sit still. Because that's what getting through something hard actually looks like — not smooth, not straight lines. Layered.

There's a verse I think of alongside this piece: "The eternal God is your dwelling place, and underneath are the everlasting arms." (Deuteronomy 33:27)

Not a promise the hard season disappears. A promise you're held inside it.
