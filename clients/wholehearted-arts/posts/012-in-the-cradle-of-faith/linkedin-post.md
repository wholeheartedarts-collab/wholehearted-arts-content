## Hook
"In the Cradle of Faith" is the name of my newest piece — and it took me a while to notice the title already says the whole thing. Faith isn't only what we reach for in a hard season. Sometimes it's what's already holding us.

## Body
This one is mixed media — acrylic and fiber on canvas, 20" x 20" — built out of vibrant color and layered, textured forms. It's not a tidy piece. Real resilience isn't tidy either. It looks like layers.

The way I described this piece when I finished it: faith as "a guiding light in moments of darkness," and even in our struggles, "a cradle of support waiting to embrace us." I keep sitting with that second part. Most of the strength I've needed in hard seasons wasn't something I found — it was something already underneath me.

There's an old verse that echoes the same image for me: "The eternal God is your dwelling place, and underneath are the everlasting arms." (Deuteronomy 33:27) Not a promise that the hard season disappears. A promise that you're held inside it.

"In the Cradle of Faith" is part of my Studio Collection — an original, 20" x 20", framed, $400.
