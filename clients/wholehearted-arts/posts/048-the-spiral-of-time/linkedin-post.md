## Hook

I don't paint time as a straight line.

My piece "The Spiral of Time" moves in circles instead — blues, ivory,
gold, and rose gathering toward a quiet center.

## Body

Past, present, and what's still ahead, folding back toward each other
rather than marching left to right in a row.

That feels closer to how time actually moves when you're living inside
it. Not a straight road. More like a spiral — the same seasons returning
in different light, the same questions circling back wiser.

Ecclesiastes puts it simply: "For everything there is a season, and a
time for every matter under heaven." Not every season arrives on a
schedule I'd choose. But I keep coming back to Psalm 31:15 when I need
to remember whose hands the clock is actually in: "My times are in your
hand."

That's the center this piece kept gathering toward. Not an answer to
"when," just a center to hold onto while time does its circling.

hear in the comments.
