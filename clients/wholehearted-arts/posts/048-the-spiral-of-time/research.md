## Executive summary

"The Spiral of Time" is an abstract Studio Collection painting built
around circular movement and the idea of past, present, and future
gathering toward a quiet center. The catalog's own description already
frames the piece as contemplative rather than explanatory — it doesn't
tell the viewer what to feel, it invites looking. That maps cleanly onto
a classic, very well-attested biblical theme: that time and seasons
belong to God, and that the believer's task is trust rather than
control. This post pairs the painting's own language (spiral, season,
center, becoming) with Ecclesiastes 3:1 and Psalm 31:15 — both verified
against ESV wording via search (direct fetches to Bible sites are
blocked in this sandbox) — without inventing any biographical story for
Agata that she hasn't stated.
