## Caption

The trees don't argue with October. They just change.

"Season of Blessings" is a grove of birches I painted in fabric and acrylic. White trunks pressed close together, a canopy going from green to gold, and a dirt path that curves off toward the fields.

I come back to it every fall. Not because it's tidy. Because it isn't.

Letting go looks messy up close. Splatter, texture, color bleeding into color. From a little distance, it's the most beautiful thing the year does.

"There is a time for everything, and a season for every activity under the heavens." (Ecclesiastes 3:1)

Whatever season you're in, the loud one or the quiet one, it counts.

What does fall look like out your window right now? Tell me in the comments.
