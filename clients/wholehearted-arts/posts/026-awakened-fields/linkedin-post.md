## Hook
Not every beautiful thing is gentle.

Some of it asks you to stay and look longer before it gives you anything back.

## Body
That's the sentence I keep coming back to with my newest piece, "Awakened Fields."

It's built from fabric and acrylic, layered until the surface has real texture — ridges and edges you can see catch the light differently depending on where you stand. I didn't want it to be easy to look at. I wanted it wild. Watchful. Full of life, the way an actual field is full of life the moment you stop walking past it and actually look.

There's a verse I keep landing on next to this one — I didn't paint it with that verse in mind, it's just where my mind goes when I sit with this piece:

"The grasslands of the wilderness overflow; the hills are clothed with gladness. The meadows are covered with flocks and the valleys are mantled with grain; they shout for joy and sing." (Psalm 65:12-13, NIV)

Wild land, overflowing anyway. Nothing tidy about it — and still glad.

That's the beauty I'm after lately. Not the kind that's easy to consume in half a second scrolling past it. The kind that asks something of you first.

"Awakened Fields" is a 16x20" original, one of one — it's the only one there'll ever be of this exact piece.
