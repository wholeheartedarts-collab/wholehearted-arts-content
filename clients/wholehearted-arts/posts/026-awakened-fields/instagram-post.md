## Caption body
This one's called "Awakened Fields."

Fabric and acrylic, built up in layers until the surface actually has texture — you can see it catch the light differently depending on where you're standing in the room.

I wanted it wild. Watchful. Full of life.

There's a psalm I keep landing on next to this piece (I didn't paint it with the verse in mind — it's just where my mind goes when I sit with it):

"The meadows are covered with flocks and the valleys are mantled with grain; they shout for joy and sing." — Psalm 65:13

Wild land, overflowing anyway. Nothing tidy about it. Still glad.

One original, 16x20", one of one.
