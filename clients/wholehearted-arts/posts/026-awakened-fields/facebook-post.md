## Hook
Not every beautiful thing is gentle — and I think that's actually the point of this one.

## Body
This is my newest piece, "Awakened Fields."

It's mixed media — fabric and acrylic, built up in layers so the surface has real texture. Depending on where you're standing, the light catches it differently. I wanted it wild. Watchful. Full of life. Some beauty is easy — you glance at it and you're done. This piece isn't that. It asks you to stay a little longer before it gives you anything back.

There's a psalm I keep landing on next to this one — I didn't paint it with that verse in mind, it's just where my mind goes when I sit with this piece:

"The meadows are covered with flocks and the valleys are mantled with grain; they shout for joy and sing." (Psalm 65:13, NIV)

Wild land, overflowing anyway. Nothing tidy about it — and still glad. That's the feeling I was after.

"Awakened Fields" is a 16x20" original, and there's only one of it. You can see it up close here: https://www.wholeheartedarts.com/atelier-collection-christian-abstract-art/p/headache
