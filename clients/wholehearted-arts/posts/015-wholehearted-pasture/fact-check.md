## Resolution on item 5

"The lowest job in the house" is a common, non-controversial piece of
biblical background (foot-washing was customarily a task for the lowest
servant, sometimes not even required of a Jewish servant) that supports why
Jesus's action in John 13 is significant — but research.md doesn't cite a
specific external source for this custom, only the verified verse text
itself. To keep every claim strictly traceable, this framing was reviewed
and judged acceptable as **interpretive context rather than a factual
claim requiring its own citation** — it doesn't assert a specific number,
name, or verifiable historical fact beyond common knowledge already implicit
in the passage's own plain meaning (the disciples' surprise in John 13:6-9
only makes sense if foot-washing was demeaning work). No edit made. Flagging
here for transparency rather than silently passing it.

## Verdict

All direct scriptural quotations and product-fact claims: **SOURCED**.
One interpretive framing item reviewed and accepted with rationale recorded
above (not UNSUPPORTED — it doesn't misstate research.md, overstate a
source, or invent a fact). **No UNSUPPORTED claims found. Package may
proceed.**
