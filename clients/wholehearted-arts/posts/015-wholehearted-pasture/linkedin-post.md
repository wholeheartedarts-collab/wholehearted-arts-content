## Hook
The lowest job in the house, in that culture, was washing feet. In John 13, Jesus takes the basin and the towel himself. That's the verse I wove into the sky of my newest small piece.

## Body
It's called "Wholehearted Pasture" — 8x10 inches, mixed media, fabric and acrylic.

John 13:5 is written into the sky: "Then he poured water into a basin and began to wash the disciples' feet and to wipe them with the towel that was wrapped around him."

He didn't delegate it. He didn't explain it first. He knelt down and did it.

A few verses later he tells them exactly why: "If I then, your Lord and Teacher, have washed your feet, you also ought to wash one another's feet. For I have given you an example, that you also should do just as I have done to you." (John 13:14-15)

I keep coming back to that when I paint — that the example wasn't a speech. It was a basin, a towel, and getting low.

This piece is small on purpose. Not every act of service needs to be a statement piece.
