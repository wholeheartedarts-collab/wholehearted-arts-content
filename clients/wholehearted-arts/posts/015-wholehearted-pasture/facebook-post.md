## Hook
The lowest job in the house, in that culture, belonged to the servant nobody noticed. In John 13, Jesus takes that job himself.

## Body
That's the verse behind my newest small piece, "Wholehearted Pasture" — 8x10 inches, fabric and acrylic, with John 13:5 written right into the sky.

"Then he poured water into a basin and began to wash the disciples' feet and to wipe them with the towel that was wrapped around him."

No speech first. No delegating it to someone else. He just knelt down with a basin and a towel.

A few verses later, he tells his disciples exactly why: "If I then, your Lord and Teacher, have washed your feet, you also ought to wash one another's feet. For I have given you an example, that you also should do just as I have done to you." (John 13:14-15)

I think about that a lot in the studio — how the clearest example of love in the whole Gospel wasn't a big gesture. It was getting low and doing the small, unglamorous thing.

This piece is small on purpose. Not everything wholehearted has to be a statement piece.
