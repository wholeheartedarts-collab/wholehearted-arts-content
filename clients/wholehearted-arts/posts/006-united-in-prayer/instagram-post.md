## Hook

"Unless the Lord builds the house, those who build it labor in vain."

That's Psalm 127:1. This piece started there.

## Caption body

This one's called United in Prayer — fabric, paper, and layers of
acrylic under a clear top coat.

It's really two prayers in one: a prayer that God would bless our house,
and a prayer for the child inside it.

By the time it was finished, it had turned into something wider. In my
own words on the piece: my prayer for all families.

I keep coming back to how much effort goes into "building" a life —
schedules, work, home, routines. This verse doesn't say stop building.
It says build on the right foundation, or it's labor for nothing.

## CTA

If you've got a house or a family you're building right now — whose
foundation is it standing on? Tell me in the comments, I'd love to know.
