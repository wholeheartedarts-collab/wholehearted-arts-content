## Hook

"Unless the Lord builds the house, those who build it labor in vain."

I've had that line — Psalm 127:1 — sitting over my worktable for weeks
now. It's the whole reason my newest piece exists.

## Body

I made "United in Prayer" out of fabric, paper, and layers of acrylic
sealed under a clear top coat. It's built around that verse, but it's
really two prayers folded into one piece: a prayer that God would bless
our house, and a prayer for the child inside it.

By the end, it had become something bigger than either of those. In my
own words on the piece: it's my prayer for all families.

I think about that a lot — how much energy goes into "building" a home.
Careers, schedules, renovations, routines. Psalm 127 doesn't say don't
build. It says build on the right foundation, or the labor is in vain.
That's not a small correction. It changes what I'm actually striving for.

This piece is signed, 24"x18", and it's one of the more directly prayerful
things I've made — less abstraction, more plea.

## CTA

If you've got a house, a family, or just a season you're trying to build
right now — whose foundation is it standing on? I'd genuinely love to
hear your answer in the comments.
