## Context: Psalm 127 as a psalm about family and home

Psalm 127 is one of the "Songs of Ascents" (Psalms 120-134), traditionally
understood as pilgrimage psalms sung on the way up to Jerusalem, and is
attributed to Solomon. Its two halves are widely read together: verses
1-2 on trusting God rather than anxious striving to build a secure home
or provide for a family, and verses 3-5 on children as a gift/heritage
from God. This is a mainstream, uncontroversial devotional reading, not a
fringe interpretation, and fits directly with the client's confirmed
pillars (faith, family, marriages, prayer) without any political or
sensitive framing.
