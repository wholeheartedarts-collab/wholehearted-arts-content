## Hook

"Unless the Lord builds the house, those who build it labor in vain."
That's Psalm 127:1, and it's the whole reason my newest piece exists.

## Body

It's called "United in Prayer" — fabric, paper, and layers of acrylic
sealed under a clear top coat, built around that verse.

It started as two prayers folded into one piece: a prayer that God would
bless our house, and a prayer for the child inside it. By the time it was
finished, it had turned into something wider. In my own words on the
piece: my prayer for all families.

I think about this a lot — how much effort goes into "building" a life.
Careers, routines, homes, schedules. Psalm 127 doesn't say stop building.
It says build on the right foundation, or the labor is for nothing. That
changes what I'm actually striving for, most days.

This one's signed, 24"x18", and it's one of the more directly prayerful
pieces I've made.

You can see it here: https://www.wholeheartedarts.com/studio-collection-agata-christian-art/p/65fi2vrmcgu28gejhaiqf5by29x62s
