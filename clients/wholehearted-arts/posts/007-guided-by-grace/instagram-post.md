## Hook

He faked madness just to survive. Then he wrote the psalm behind "taste
and see that the Lord is good."

## Caption body

Psalm 34 — the scripture worked into this piece — wasn't written from a
comfortable place.

David wrote it after escaping a Philistine city by pretending to be
insane, spit running down his beard, one step ahead of losing his life.
From that cave, he wrote: "Taste and see that the LORD is good."

Not after things settled down. In the middle of it.

"Guided by Grace" holds that same tension — an illuminated figure at the
center, Psalm 34's words worked into the canvas itself. Hope and strength
held together, not one after the other.
