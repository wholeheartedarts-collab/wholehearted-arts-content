## Hook
Waiting isn't the same as giving up.

"Wait for the LORD; be strong, and let your heart take courage." —
Psalm 27:14

## Caption body
I keep coming back to that verse. Twice in one line, it says wait —
but both times it's paired with be strong. Not sit still. Be strong.

This piece is called "Patience."

Layer after layer of texture and textile, soft movement running
through it, luminous circles gathering around a quiet figure at the
center. None of that happens fast. It has to be built, slowly, one
layer at a time.

That's what real patience looks like to me — not passive, not stuck.
Still forming. Still strong.
