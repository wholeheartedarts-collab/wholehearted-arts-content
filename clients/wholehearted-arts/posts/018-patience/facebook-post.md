## Hook
"Wait for the LORD; be strong, and let your heart take courage; wait
for the LORD!" — Psalm 27:14

## Body
I've been sitting with this verse for a while. Twice in one line, it
says wait. But both times, it's paired with be strong — not sit still,
not give up. Be strong.

I think we sometimes treat patience like it's just delay — like nothing
is really happening while we wait. This verse doesn't read that way to
me at all.

That's the feeling behind my newest piece, simply titled "Patience." It's
built in layers — texture, textile, acrylic, worked slowly, with soft
movement running through it and luminous circles gathering around a
quiet, contemplative figure at the center. None of that comes together
in one sitting. It has to be built, one layer at a time, the same way
patience does.

If you're in a season of waiting right now, I hope this piece is a
reminder: waiting isn't nothing. It's still being formed.
