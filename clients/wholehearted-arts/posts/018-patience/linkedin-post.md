## Body
Most people think patience means passivity — sitting still until
something changes.

I don't think that's true. Not in scripture, and not in the studio.

Psalm 27:14 says, "Wait for the LORD; be strong, and let your heart
take courage; wait for the LORD!" That's not resignation. That's an
instruction to be strong — twice, in one verse, with waiting bracketed
on both sides by it.

My newest piece, "Patience," is built the way I think real patience
looks: layer over layer of texture and textile, with soft movement
running through it and luminous circular forms gathering around a
quiet, contemplative figure at the center. That kind of layering can't
happen in one sitting. It has to be built.

There's a kind of stillness that isn't waiting for permission to move —
it's already strong, already steady, just not finished yet.

That's the piece I made this time.
