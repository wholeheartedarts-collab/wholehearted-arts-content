## Hook
I paint marriage with circles and squares.

Not because it's neat symbolism — because it's true to how hard, and how good, it actually is.

## Body
In "The Strength of Three Strands," the circles are the feminine. The squares are the masculine. I mix them on purpose, because that's the work: I have to become a greater circle to fit into the square, and the square has to grow to make room for me. Neither of us gets to stay the same shape.

That's not a compromise I came up with. It's from Ecclesiastes 4:12 — "a cord of three strands is not quickly broken." Two people holding on to each other is strong. Two people holding on to each other *and* to God is a different kind of strong.

This piece is my attempt to paint that third strand — the one you can't always see, but the one that keeps the other two from fraying.

It's mixed media on canvas, unframed, the kind of piece that asks you to stand close before it makes sense.

## CTA
If a season of your own marriage, family, or friendship has needed that third strand lately — I'd love to hear about it in the comments.
