## Hook
Circles are the feminine. Squares are the masculine. I paint them mixed on purpose.

## Caption body
Neither shape gets to stay comfortable.

I have to become a greater circle to fit into the square. The square has to grow to make room for me. That's the whole marriage, right there, before you even get to the hard days.

"The Strength of Three Strands" comes from Ecclesiastes 4:12 — a cord of three strands is not quickly broken. Two people holding on to each other is strong. Two people holding on to each other and to God is a different kind of strong.

I painted the third strand as the one you can't always see in a marriage, but the one that keeps the other two from fraying.
