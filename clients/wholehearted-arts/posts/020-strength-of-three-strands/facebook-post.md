## Hook
A strong marriage isn't a two-person project. I don't think a strong friendship or family is, either.

## Body
That's what I was thinking about while painting "The Strength of Three Strands." It comes from Ecclesiastes 4:12: "a cord of three strands is not quickly broken." Two people holding on to each other can hold up a lot. Two people holding on to each other and to God can hold up more.

In the painting, I used circles for the feminine and squares for the masculine, and I mixed them together on purpose. Neither one gets to stay in its own comfortable shape. I have to become a bigger circle to fit into the square. The square has to grow to make room for me. That's true of marriage, and honestly it's true of any relationship worth keeping — family, close friendships, the people you pray with.

The third strand is the one you can't always see. It's also the one that keeps the other two from fraying.
