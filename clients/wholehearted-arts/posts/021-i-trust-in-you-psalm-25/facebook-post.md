## Hook
I wanted to paint trust the way it actually feels — not after things work out, but before.

## Body
That's what "I Trust In You My God" is about. It comes from Psalm 25, and instead of painting the psalm as a background or a caption, I wrote its words directly into the figure — into the shape of her body and the folds of her clothing. The prayer isn't next to her. It's what she's made of.

Psalm 25 opens with David saying, "In you, LORD my God, I put my trust." Not once he knew how things would turn out. Right at the start, before any of that was clear.

Further into the same psalm, he asks for something different than rescue: "Show me your ways, LORD, teach me your paths." Not "fix this for me." "Teach me." I think that's the harder, more honest prayer — and it's the one I wanted this piece to hold onto.

It's mixed media — acrylic, alcohol ink, pen, and fabric — on a 20x20 canvas.

## CTA
Which one do you find yourself praying more — asking to be rescued, or asking to be taught? I'd love to hear your thoughts in the comments.
