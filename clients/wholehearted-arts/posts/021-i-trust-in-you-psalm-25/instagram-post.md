## Hook
The words aren't written under her. They're written into her.

## Caption body
Psalm 25, handwritten into the shape of a woman and the folds of her clothing. Not a caption on the painting — the material of it.

"In you, LORD my God, I put my trust." That's the opening line. Trust offered first, before anything is resolved.

Later in the same psalm: "Show me your ways, LORD, teach me your paths." Not "get me out of this." "Teach me." That's the harder prayer, and the one this piece is really about.

Mixed media — acrylic, alcohol ink, pen, fabric — on canvas, 20x20.
