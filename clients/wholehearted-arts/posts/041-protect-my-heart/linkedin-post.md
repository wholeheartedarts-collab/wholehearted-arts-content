## Hook
There's a woman standing guard in my newest painting, "Protect My Heart." I didn't paint her closed off. I painted her choosing what gets in.

## Body
"Protect My Heart" is oil on canvas, framed, 18"x24". A woman as the guardian of her own heart. Not a fortress. A guardian.

There's a difference, and it's the whole reason I painted her the way I did.

Proverbs 4:23 says, "Above all else, guard your heart, for everything you do flows from it." (NIV)

I used to read that verse as a wall-building instruction. Protect yourself. Don't let anyone close enough to hurt you again.

I don't read it that way anymore. Guarding isn't the same as closing. You can shield your heart from what wants to wreck you and still leave the door open for what's actually good - connection, love, the people who've earned a place there.

That's the whole painting. Shield and open. Both, not either.

## CTA
If you've ever had to relearn the difference between guarding your heart and shutting it down, I'd love to hear where that showed up for you.
