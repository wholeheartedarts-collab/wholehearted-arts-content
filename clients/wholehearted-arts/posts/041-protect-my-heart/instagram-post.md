## Caption body
"Protect My Heart" - oil on canvas, 18"x24", framed.

Proverbs 4:23: "Above all else, guard your heart, for everything you do flows from it."

I used to read that as armor up, let no one near.

Now I think guarding and staying open are the same instruction, not opposites. You shield what matters. You still leave the door open for what's actually good.

That tension is the whole painting.
