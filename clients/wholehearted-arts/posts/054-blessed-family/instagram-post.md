## Caption body
This little one is called "Blessed Family."

7"x10". Unframed. Acrylic and mica powder — it catches the light differently depending on where you stand.

I kept thinking about Psalm 128 while I made it:

"Your wife will be like a fruitful vine within your house; your children will be like olive shoots around your table." (Psalm 128:3, ESV)

Not a promise of an easy life. Just — gathered. Still at the table. Still walking the same direction.

Small piece. Big idea.
