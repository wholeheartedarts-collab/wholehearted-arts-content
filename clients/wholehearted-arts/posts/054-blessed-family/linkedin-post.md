## Hook
There's a psalm that doesn't promise wealth, success, or an easy life. It promises a table — and the people gathered around it. That's the whole blessing.

## Body
I just finished a small piece called "Blessed Family." Seven inches by ten. Unframed. Acrylic and mica powder, so it catches the light differently depending on where you're standing.

It's not a grand piece. It's quiet. And the smallness is kind of the point.

I keep coming back to Psalm 128 while I think about it:

"Blessed is everyone who fears the LORD, who walks in his ways! ... Your wife will be like a fruitful vine within your house; your children will be like olive shoots around your table." (Psalm 128:1,3, ESV)

No olive trees in my studio. But I know what that image is pointing at — a home that's blessed not because everything in it is impressive, but because the people in it are still gathered, still at the table, still walking the same direction together.

That's harder to paint than it sounds. Easier to say than to live, some days.
