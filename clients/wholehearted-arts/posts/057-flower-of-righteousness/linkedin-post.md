## Hook
There's a way I see the world that I don't talk about much. Small
particles, constantly moving, never still — yet somehow holding
together. I think it has to do with my vision. I know it shows up in my
painting.

## Body
That's the honest truth behind a small piece I made called "Flower of
Righteousness."

It's unframed, only 11 by 9 inches, mixed media on paper. Nothing
showy. But it carries Philippians 1:9-11 — Paul's prayer that love
would keep abounding, "with knowledge and all discernment," so that
what's left in us is clear and whole.

That's genuinely how I see things, most of the time: particles.
Fragments. Nothing in my field of vision ever fully sits still — but
somehow, it all spins into one thing. Holding on to itself. Holding on
to each other.

I don't think that's just about my eyes. I think that's what
discernment actually looks like, most days. Not perfect stillness.
Fragments that choose, again and again, to hold together.

This piece is sold out now. I'm not sharing it to sell it. I'm sharing
it because I don't say this part out loud very often, and today felt
like the day to.

## CTA
