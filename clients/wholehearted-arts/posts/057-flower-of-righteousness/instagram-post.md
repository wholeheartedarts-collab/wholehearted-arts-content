## Hook
I don't usually say this part out loud: this is genuinely how I see
the world. Small particles. Always moving. Somehow holding together.

## Caption body
This little piece is called "Flower of Righteousness." Mixed media,
unframed, only 11x9 inches. It carries Philippians 1:9-11 — Paul's
prayer that love would keep abounding, "with knowledge and all
discernment."

When I look at anything, it's never quite still for me. Particles.
Fragments. Spinning. And somehow, it all becomes one thing anyway —
holding on to itself, holding on to the next piece.

I think that's discernment, most days. Not stillness. Fragments that
choose to hold together.

This one's already sold out, so I'm not posting to sell it — just to
share the part of how I see that doesn't usually make it into a
caption.
