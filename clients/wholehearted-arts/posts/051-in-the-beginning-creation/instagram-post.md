## Hook
Before there was light, there was a spiral of words.

## Caption body
"In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth."

I wrote that verse — Genesis 1:1-2 — right into this piece. Not in a straight line. In a spiral.

Because I don't think creation was a one-and-done thing. I think it's still turning. Still shaping the formless parts. Including the formless parts in me.

If something in your life feels unfinished right now — still waiting, still taking shape — maybe that's not a sign God's not working. Maybe that's exactly where He starts.
