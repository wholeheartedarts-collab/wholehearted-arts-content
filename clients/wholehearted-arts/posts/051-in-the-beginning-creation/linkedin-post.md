## Hook
"In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth."

Before there was form. Before there was light. That's the line I keep coming back to in my new piece.

## Body
This one started with four words I've read a hundred times and still can't get used to: *in the beginning.*

Genesis opens with the earth formless, empty, dark — and then God starts speaking order into it. Not building from a blueprint. Just... beginning.

I wrote the actual words of Genesis 1:1-2 into this piece, worked into a spiral instead of a straight line. A spiral doesn't end where it starts. It keeps turning outward. The more I sat with that shape, the more it felt right — because I don't think creation was a one-time event God finished and moved on from. I think it's still happening. In the "formless and void" parts of our own lives, too.

If something in your life feels unfinished, unshaped, still waiting — that's not evidence God's done. It might be exactly where He starts.
