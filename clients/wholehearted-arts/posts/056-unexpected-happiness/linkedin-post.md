## Hook
I named a painting "Unexpected Happiness" on purpose. Every word in that title is doing work.

Unexpected. Undeserved. Happy.

## Body
This piece is a refusal, in color. It's what happens when you decide not to accept the grey reality of a failure as the final answer — and then, right when you'd stopped expecting anything else, hope shows up instead.

That's not a small thing to paint. Most of us know what it is to brace for the grey outcome.

Scripture already has language for this, and it's older and steadier than anything I could add:

Weeping may tarry for the night, but joy comes with the morning. (Psalm 30:5, ESV)

Night first. Then morning — not because we talked ourselves into it, but because it was always coming.

I don't think I'm the only one who braces for the grey outcome more often than I'd like to admit.
