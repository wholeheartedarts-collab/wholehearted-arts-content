## Hook
I named this one "Unexpected Happiness" on purpose.

Unexpected. Undeserved. Happy.

## Caption body
This piece is a refusal, in color.

A refusal to let the grey reality of a failure be the final answer.

And then — right when I'd stopped expecting anything else — hope showed up anyway.

Weeping may tarry for the night,
but joy comes with the morning.
(Psalm 30:5, ESV)

Night first. Then morning.
Not because we talked ourselves into it.
Because it was always coming.
