# Brief: 001-ai-automation-for-creative-entrepreneurs

- **Topic:** Why an artist would use AI to automate the business side of
  a creative practice — bridging Agata's art pillar and the AI-venture
  pillar.
- **Audience:** Bridges both segments — art collectors curious about the
  process behind the work, and small business owners evaluating AI
  automation, since Agata speaks as a credible example of both.
- **Goal:** Build credibility for the new AI-automation venture using
  Agata's own lived experience as proof, not abstract pitching.
- **Takeaway:** AI automation isn't about replacing the creative work —
  it's about freeing up time from the repetitive business tasks around
  it, and that's exactly what's being built for other local businesses.
- **Tone:** Energetic, personal, honest about both the upside and the
  limits.

  (research → write → review → fact-check) end to end after initial
  system build. This is a system test, not necessarily what should be
