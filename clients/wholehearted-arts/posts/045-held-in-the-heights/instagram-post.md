## Hook
Some places look rugged until the light touches them.
Then you realize — they were never empty.

## Caption body
"Held in the Hights" (typo's on my own website, not here 😅 — it's "Heights," and the piece itself gets that right).

20x20. Mixed textures, built to catch light differently depending on where you stand.

While I was finishing it I kept coming back to Habakkuk 3:19:

"GOD, the Lord, is my strength; he makes my feet like the deer's; he makes me tread on my high places."

Habakkuk doesn't start this book calm — he opens it asking God hard questions about injustice and waiting. By chapter 3, he's still on the high, difficult ground. He just has his footing now.

That's what I wanted this piece to hold. Not a soft landscape. A rugged one, that turns out to be exactly where the grace was.
