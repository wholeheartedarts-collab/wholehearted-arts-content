## Caption body

"Seeking His Will" — mixed media, acrylic, fabric, a little mica for
shimmer, in an old restored frame I let keep its wear and tear.

The verse running through it is Psalm 31:14-15:

"But I trust in you, O LORD; I say, 'You are my God.' My times are in
your hand."

Not my plans. My times.

That's the part I keep coming back to on the weeks when I don't know
what's next — in the studio or anywhere else.
