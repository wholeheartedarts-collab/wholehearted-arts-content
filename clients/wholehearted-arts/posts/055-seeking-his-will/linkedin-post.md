## Hook

There's a small piece on my worktable right now with Psalm 31 written
straight into the paint. Not printed. Not stenciled on after. Written in,
while the piece was still becoming itself.

## Body

I called it "Seeking His Will."

It's mixed media — acrylic, fabric, a little mica for shimmer — set in an
old restored frame that still has some wear and tear on it. I didn't try
to hide that. Some frames are supposed to look like they've held
something for a while.

The verse running through it is Psalm 31:14-15:

"But I trust in you, O LORD; I say, 'You are my God.' My times are in
your hand."

I think about that line more than almost any other when I'm deciding
what to paint next, what to charge, whether to keep going on a hard week
in this new chapter of my work. Not "my plans are in your hand" —
my times. The waiting counts too. The not-knowing-yet counts too.

I don't always get to decide when the breakthrough comes. I do get to
decide whether I keep seeking His will while I wait for it.

That's what this piece is actually about. Not a tidy resolution — a
posture.
