## Executive summary

"Seeking His Will" is a small mixed-media piece (acrylic, fabric, mica)
with Psalm 31 worked directly into the surface, set in an old restored
frame with visible wear. The piece pairs naturally with Psalm 31:14-15 —
"But I trust in you, O LORD... my times are in your hand" — a verse about
trusting God's timing and will rather than one's own plans. This fits the
client's confirmed pillar of "overcoming struggles by staying faithful to
God" and the practice of putting scripture physically into the work.

## Key points

- The painting's own description states "Psalm 31 written into it" —
  this is a verified catalog fact, not an inference.
- Psalm 31 (ESV) is a prayer of David expressing trust in God as refuge
  and "rock and fortress," and asking God to guide him for the sake of
  His name (v.3), with a turn toward surrender in v.14-15: "But I trust in
  you, O LORD; I say, 'You are my God.' My times are in your hand."
  (Confirmed via WebSearch result snippets from esv.org and bible.com,
  since esv.org/biblegateway.com/biblehub.com are blocked by sandbox
  egress — see note below.)
- The piece is physically "Old Restored Frame (have some wear and tear)"
  — a concrete, sensory detail straight from the catalog, usable in the
  post per brand-voice's preference for concrete/sensory detail over
  abstraction.
- No artist statement exists in the catalog for this piece beyond
  medium/size/scripture reference — per skip_rules, keep the post
  shorter and image-led rather than fabricating a personal story about
  this specific piece.
