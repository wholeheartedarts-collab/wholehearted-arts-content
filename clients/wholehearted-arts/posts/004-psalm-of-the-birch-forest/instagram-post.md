## Hook
The heavens declare the glory of God without saying a single word. I wanted to paint what that looks like.

## Caption body
This is "Psalm of the Birch Forest" — birch trunks with verses from Psalm 19 written right into the bark.

"The heavens declare the glory of God; the skies proclaim the work of his hands... their words to the ends of the world." (Psalm 19:1-4)

No sound. No sermon. Just trees, standing there, telling the truth anyway.

I layered fabric and acrylic into the canvas so the texture itself carries weight — rough where the bark is rough, soft where the light breaks through.

40"×30", framed and ready for a wall that needs a quiet reminder.
