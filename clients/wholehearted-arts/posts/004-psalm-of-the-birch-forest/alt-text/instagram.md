Alt text: Straight-on close-up of a richly textured mixed-media painting of a birch forest in autumn, framed in black and gold. Printed scripture text runs vertically down several trunks and is clearly legible, including the phrases "the law of the LORD is perfect, reviving the soul," "the precepts of the LORD are right, rejoicing the [heart]," "the fear of the LORD is clean, enduring forever," and "more to be desired are they [than gold]" — verses from Psalm 19 written directly into the bark. The painted surface has heavy, dimensional texture from built-up acrylic and layered paper.
