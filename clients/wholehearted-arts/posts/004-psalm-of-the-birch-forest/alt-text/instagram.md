Alt text: Close-up detail of a richly textured mixed-media painting of a birch forest in autumn. Vertical black-and-white birch trunks rise through layers of acrylic paint and collaged paper in gold, orange, green, purple, and rust. A dark red-brown branch curves diagonally across the upper half, dotted with small clusters of yellow, green, and orange paint. Fragments of printed text are visible collaged into one of the trunks, including the legible phrase "...as se[t] a tent for the s[un]..." — part of a Psalm 19 verse written directly into the bark. The surface has heavy, dimensional texture from built-up paint and paper.
