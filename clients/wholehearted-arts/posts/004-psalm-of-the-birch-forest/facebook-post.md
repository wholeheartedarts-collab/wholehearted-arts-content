## Hook
Look closely at this one — the trees are actually saying something.

## Body
This is "Psalm of the Birch Forest," and if you get close to the canvas, you'll see it: verses from Psalm 19 written right into the birch trunks.

"The heavens declare the glory of God; the skies proclaim the work of his hands... they have no speech, they use no words... yet their voice goes out into all the earth." (Psalm 19:1-4)

That's the whole idea of this piece. Creation doesn't need words to testify — so I gave it words anyway, layered into fabric and acrylic, trunk by trunk, until the forest itself is quietly preaching.

It's 40"×30", mixed media, from my Atelier Collection, and it's one of my favorite pieces to just stand in front of.
