## Hook
I wrote scripture into a forest.

Not next to it. Not below it as a caption. Into the bark itself, trunk by trunk, until you have to slow down and read the trees to find it.

## Body
This is "Psalm of the Birch Forest" — 40"×30", mixed media, part of my Atelier Collection.

The idea started with Psalm 19:

"The heavens declare the glory of God; the skies proclaim the work of his hands. Day after day they pour forth speech; night after night they reveal knowledge. They have no speech, they use no words; no sound is heard from them. Yet their voice goes out into all the earth." (Psalm 19:1-4, NIV)

Creation testifies without saying a word. So I made that literal. I layered fabric and acrylic into birch trunks and wrote lines from the psalm directly into the bark — you have to get close to read them, the same way you have to get quiet to notice most of what creation is actually saying.

It's a slow piece. It doesn't explain itself from across the room. You have to stand in front of it.
