### Ephesians 5:25-33 — New International Version (NIV)

**Verses 25-33 (full text)**

"Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ loved the church and gave himself up for her to make her holy, cleansing her by the washing with water through the word, and to present her to himself as a radiant church, without stain or wrinkle or any other blemish, but holy and blameless. In this same way, husbands ought to love their wives as their own bodies. He who loves his wife loves himself. After all, no one ever hated their own body, but they feed and care for their body, just as Christ does the church—for we are members of his body. For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and be united to his wife, and the two will become one flesh. This is a profound mystery—but I am talking about Christ and the church. However, each one of you also must love his wife as he loves himself, and the wife must respect her husband."

## Polish Marriage Award — STATUS: VERIFIED

**Claim**: "In Poland, people who are married for 50 years receive a medal from the president." — Confirmed accurate.

- **Official name**: Medal za Długoletnie Pożycie Małżeńskie ("Medal for Long Marital Life")
- **Established**: 1960
- **Awarded by**: The President of Poland, to couples married 50 continuous years (presentation is often delegated locally — voivodes, mayors, marshals — but the honor is presidential)
- **Process**: Not automatic; initiated by the local Civil Registry Office, application forwarded to the President via the voivode
- **Source**: Official site of the President of Poland — https://www.prezydent.pl/prezydent/kompetencje/ordery-i-odznaczenia/odznaczenia/medal-za-dlugoletnie-pozycie-malzenskie
- **Source**: Wikipedia (English) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medal_for_Long_Marital_Life
- **Source**: Wikipedia (Polish) — https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medal_za_D%C5%82ugoletnie_Po%C5%BCycie_Ma%C5%82%C5%BCe%C5%84skie
- **Accessed**: 2026-08-06
