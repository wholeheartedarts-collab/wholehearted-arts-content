Alt text: A framed mixed-media painting titled "Enduring Love; Strength through Trials," hung on a light wood-slat wall with matching tan padding on either side of the frame. The painting shows an elderly couple embracing: a bald man with closed eyes and a gentle smile rests his cheek against a gray-haired woman's head, his arm draped over her shoulder. The woman, in a magenta and pink patterned dress with swirling embroidered motifs, leans into his chest, her long silver hair flowing sideways across the canvas. Behind them, a colorful stylized skyline of stacked, patchwork-colored buildings rises up, with a tall spired temple structure suggesting Jerusalem, set against a pale blue textured sky.
