## Caption

Not everything urgent is yours to carry.

I have to say that out loud some weeks. The phone, the list, the things that could go wrong. They all arrive wearing the same uniform, like every one of them is an emergency.

Most of them aren't.

"Do not be anxious about anything, but in every situation, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God. And the peace of God, which transcends all understanding, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus." (Philippians 4:6-7)

Guard. Not explain, not fix. Guard.

The blur behind these words is my painting "Silent, I Held My Peace." A woman with her eyes closed, holding still inside a lot of noise. She isn't calm because the noise stopped. She's calm inside it.
