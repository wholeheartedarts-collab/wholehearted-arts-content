## Hook

I wrote this verse by hand, straight onto the canvas: "bone of my bones and flesh of my flesh."

## Caption body

This one's "Love Her Like Yourself" — 20x20, mixed media.

The words are Genesis 2:21-24. The deep sleep. The rib. The moment the man wakes up and recognizes her — not as his opposite, but as the same substance he is.

"A man shall leave his father and his mother and hold fast to his wife, and they shall become one flesh."

The title comes from a different verse entirely — the one that says a husband already loves his wife's wellbeing like he loves his own body, because that's literally what she is to him now. I didn't write that verse on the canvas, but it's the whole reason this piece exists.

One flesh isn't a feeling you wait to arrive. It's a fact you grow into living like it's true.
