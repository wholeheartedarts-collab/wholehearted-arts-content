## Hook

I wrote a Bible verse directly onto a 20"x20" canvas, in the same hand I use to sign my name.

It's the verse about where "one flesh" actually comes from — and it's not the verse most people quote when they talk about marriage.

## Body

This one's called "Love Her Like Yourself."

The words on the canvas are Genesis 2:21-24 — the deep sleep, the rib, the moment the man wakes up and says, "This at last is bone of my bones and flesh of my flesh." Then: "a man shall leave his father and his mother and hold fast to his wife, and they shall become one flesh."

I didn't pick that passage because it's romantic. I picked it because it's specific. She isn't described as his opposite or his project. She's described as made of the same substance he is — "bone of my bones" — before anyone says a word about roles or responsibilities.

The title comes from somewhere else in scripture: the instruction for a husband to love his wife as he loves his own body, because that's what she already is to him. Not a separate verse written on this canvas — just the idea the whole piece is built around.

I think that's the actual argument for "one flesh." Not a feeling. A fact about what you already share, that you then have to live like it's true.
