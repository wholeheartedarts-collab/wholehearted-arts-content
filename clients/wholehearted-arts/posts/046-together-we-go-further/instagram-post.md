## Hook
There was a season I couldn't see the world clearly. What carried me through? My friends.

## Caption body
I painted "Together we go further" in the middle of that season.

I couldn't see clearly, but I could feel it — friends walking close enough that my steps stayed secure even when my sight wasn't.

That's not just a nice memory to me. That's God loving me through my friends.

"Two are better than one... for if they fall, one will lift up his fellow." (Ecclesiastes 4:9-10)

Acrylic on paper, 22x30. Made in gratitude, not in theory.

## CTA
Who's walked with you through a season like that? Tell me in the comments — I'd love to hear.
