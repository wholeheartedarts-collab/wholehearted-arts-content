## Hook
There was a season when I couldn't see the world clearly. Not a metaphor — literally. And the thing that carried me through wasn't my own strength. It was my friends.

## Body
I made "Together we go further" during that season.

I couldn't see the world clearly, but I could still feel it — and what I felt most was people staying close. Friends who walked with me so steadily that my steps stayed secure, even when my sight wasn't.

Looking back, I don't experience that as just a nice season with good friends. I experience it as God loving me *through* my friends. Their presence was the love, delivered in a form I could actually receive.

There's a verse that's stayed with me since: "Two are better than one, because they have a good reward for their toil... for if they fall, one will lift up his fellow" (Ecclesiastes 4:9-10). It's not a sentimental line about friendship in general. It's specifically about what happens when one person stumbles and someone else is close enough to catch it.

That's the painting. Acrylic on paper, 22x30 — made in gratitude, not in theory.

## CTA
