## Hook
Mothers' tears are never wasted.

## Caption body
I built this one with a palette knife — layer over layer, nothing
smoothed flat. You can see it in the surface: grief doesn't just
disappear. It gets worked into something with shape.

"Where She Cried Flower Grew" started as a conviction, not a title. As
mothers, we carry grief for our children and keep working toward
something better for them — wherever we're from, whatever we look like.

Psalm 126:5 says it plainly:
"Those who sow in tears shall reap with shouts of joy!"

Not even the tears you cried alone are wasted.
