## Hook
Mothers' tears are never wasted.

That line kept surfacing while I worked on my newest piece, and I
couldn't shake it until I'd actually painted it.

## Body
"Where She Cried Flower Grew" started there — as a conviction, not just
a title. As mothers, we carry grief for our children's sake, and we keep
working toward something better for them, no matter where we live or
what we look like.

I built this one with a palette knife — structural, layered, not
smoothed flat. It felt right for the subject. Grief doesn't just
disappear. It gets worked into something with shape, over time.

Scripture has been saying this longer than I have. Psalm 126:5 puts it
plainly: "Those who sow in tears shall reap with shouts of joy!" And
Psalm 56:8 goes even further — God doesn't just let tears pass by
unnoticed. He counts them: "You have kept count of my tossings; put my
tears in your bottle. Are they not in your book?"

Nothing felt in sorrow is wasted. Not even the tears you cried alone.
