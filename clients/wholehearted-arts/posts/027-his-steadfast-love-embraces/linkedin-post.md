## Hook
There's a psalm worked directly into this painting — not painted over it as decoration, but stitched into the piece itself.

## Body
The piece is called "His Steadfast Love Embraces," from my Studio Collection. It's small — 8x10 inches, fabric and acrylic on canvas board, framed to about 11.5x14.5.

The scripture is Psalm 119:41-48. It opens with a plea: "Let your steadfast love come to me, O Lord, your salvation according to your promise." A few verses later: "I shall walk in a wide place, for I have sought your precepts."

That's the phrase that stays with me — steadfast love making room. Not love that fixes everything on the spot, but love that's simply there, and stays there, wide enough to walk in.

This one's smaller and quieter than some of my other work. Some pieces carry a whole story. This one carries a handful of verses and a lot of texture.

## CTA
If you've ever needed a promise to just stay put long enough to let you breathe — I'd love to know which line of that passage lands for you.
