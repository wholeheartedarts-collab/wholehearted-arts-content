## Caption body
"His Steadfast Love Embraces" — mixed media, fabric and acrylic on canvas board, 8x10 inches.

Psalm 119:41-48 lives inside it.

"Let your steadfast love come to me, O Lord, your salvation according to your promise..."

...and a little further in: "I shall walk in a wide place."

Small piece. A lot of room to breathe.
