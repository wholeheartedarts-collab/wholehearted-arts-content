## Hook
This little painting has a psalm stitched right into it.

## Body
"His Steadfast Love Embraces" is one of the newer pieces in my Studio Collection — mixed media, fabric and acrylic on canvas board, just 8x10 inches before framing.

The verses are Psalm 119:41-48. It opens with a prayer — "Let your steadfast love come to me, O Lord, your salvation according to your promise" — and a little further in: "I shall walk in a wide place, for I have sought your precepts."

That phrase has stayed with me while sitting with this one: steadfast love making room to walk in. Not a fix, not an instant answer — just room.

It's a small piece, but it says something I keep coming back to.
