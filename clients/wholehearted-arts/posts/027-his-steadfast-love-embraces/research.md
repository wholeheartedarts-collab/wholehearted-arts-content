# Research Brief: "His Steadfast Love Embraces" (Studio Collection)

## Executive summary

"His Steadfast Love Embraces" is a small mixed-media piece (fabric and
acrylic on canvas board, 8"x10", framed to 11.5"x14.5") from Agata
May'kowska's Studio Collection, listed at $150.00 with Psalm 119:41-48
written directly into the work. The passage is the "Waw" stanza of
Psalm 119 — a prayer for God's steadfast love (Hebrew *hesed*) and
faithfulness to sustain the psalmist against mockery and doubt, and a
resolve to keep delighting in God's word regardless. This gives the
post a clean, low-risk angle: the scripture is not an editorial pairing
invented for the post, it is literally part of the painting, so the
post can say so plainly.

## Key points

- The painting incorporates Psalm 119:41-48 (ESV or a similar
  translation — the exact translation stitched/painted into the piece
  is not stated in the catalog and should not be assumed; refer to it
  generically as "Psalm 119:41-48" rather than quoting a specific
  translation as what's literally on the canvas).
- Psalm 119 is the longest chapter in the Bible, an acrostic built from
  the 22 letters of the Hebrew alphabet, each 8-verse stanza beginning
  with the same letter. Verses 41-48 form the "Waw" stanza.
- Core content of 41-48: a plea for God's steadfast love (*hesed*) and
  salvation to "come to me" (v.41); it gives the psalmist "an answer for
  him who taunts me" because his trust is in God's word (v.42); a
  resolve to keep the word "continually, forever and ever" (v.44); a
  sense of walking "in a wide place" — freedom — because he has sought
  God's precepts (v.45); a resolve to speak of God's testimonies "before
  kings" without shame (v.46); and delight in, and lifted hands toward,
  God's commandments (vv.47-48).
- Medium: mixed media (fabric + acrylic on canvas board) — a textured,
  tactile piece rather than a flat painted surface, consistent with the
  "layered, textural" character of several Studio Collection works.
- Size: small — 8"x10" unframed, 11.5"x14.5" framed (as shown in the
  listing's third photo).
- Price: $150.00 USD. Stock: 1 in stock (per catalog snapshot).
- No caution_note, price_note, or availability_note flagged on this
  catalog entry.
- No `chosen_images` block exists for this entry (unlike some other
  catalog entries where a human already reviewed every photo and

## Possible content angles

1. **The scripture as the centerpiece** — lead with the fact that
   Psalm 119:41-48 is literally part of the painting (not an editorial
   pairing), and let one or two lines of the passage (steadfast love,
   walking in "a wide place") carry the emotional weight.
2. **Steadfast love as the throughline** — focus on the phrase
   "steadfast love" (Hebrew *hesed*) itself: covenant loyalty that
   doesn't waver, and how that idea shows up in a small, intimate,
   textured piece.
3. **"Walking in a wide place" as freedom** — angle on verse 45's image
   of spaciousness/freedom that comes from seeking God's precepts, tying
   to the "overcoming struggles by staying faithful" content pillar.
4. **Process/medium angle** — the fact that this is mixed media (fabric
   + acrylic), a smaller and more intimate scale than some Studio
   pieces, inviting a "come close and look" framing appropriate for a
   $150 piece.
5. **Quiet resolve angle** — verses 46-48's resolve to speak without
   shame and delight in God's word, a steadier and less "burdened" tone
   than some past posts (helpful for pillar variety after several posts
   that leaned on struggle/burden themes, e.g. posts 025-026).

Recommended primary angle for this run: **#1 combined with #2** — lead
with the fact that the psalm is physically part of the piece, and let
"steadfast love" be the emotional anchor, since it's both the painting's
title and the passage's opening phrase. Keep the post shorter and
image-led per the thin-narrative skip rule.
