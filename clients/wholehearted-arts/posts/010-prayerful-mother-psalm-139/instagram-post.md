## Hook
A prayer for a mom and a child, painted.
The wonder of pregnancy — and the worry too.

## Caption body
I made "Prayerful Mother - Psalm 139" thinking about how much of loving a child is just... trusting what you can't see yet.

Psalm 139 says it best: "In your book were written, every one of them, the days that were formed for me, when as yet there was none of them." (v.16)

He has a good plan. All is written in his book.

I worked the psalm's own words into the piece — not as decoration around the edges, but woven into the painting itself. A prayer you can hang on a wall.
