## Hook
Nobody tells you how much of parenting is just trusting what you can't see yet.

## Body
That's what I was sitting with when I painted "Prayerful Mother - Psalm 139." It's a prayer for a mom and a child — for the wonder of pregnancy, and honestly, the worry that comes right alongside it.

Psalm 139 says: "In your book were written, every one of them, the days that were formed for me, when as yet there was none of them." Every day already known. Already held.

He has a good plan. All is written in his book.

I worked those words right into the painting itself, so the prayer isn't just something you read once — it's part of the piece, every time you look at it.
