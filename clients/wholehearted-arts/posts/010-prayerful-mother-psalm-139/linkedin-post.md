## Hook
Before I painted a single stroke of "Prayerful Mother," Psalm 139 had already said it: "In your book were written, every one of them, the days that were formed for me, when as yet there was none of them."

## Body
That's verse 16. Most people only know verse 14 — "fearfully and wonderfully made." I love that line too. But it was verse 16 that shaped this painting.

"Prayerful Mother - Psalm 139" is a prayer for a mom and a child. I made it while sitting with the wonder of pregnancy — and the worry that comes with it too. Nobody prepares you for how much of parenting is just... not knowing.

Not knowing who this person will become. Not knowing what they'll face. Not knowing if you're doing any of it right.

Psalm 139 doesn't answer any of that. What it says instead is that the days are already written. Formed. Held. Before any of them happened.

I worked the words of that psalm right into the piece — not as decoration, but because I wanted the prayer to be physically part of the object, not just a caption underneath it.
