Alt text: A square, gold-framed mixed media painting titled "Fear Not" hangs on a white wall in a bright loft-style living room with exposed brick columns, a light gray sofa, and green potted plants on either side. The painting itself is richly abstract and layered: sweeping red curved lines arc across the right side over a textured field of teal, turquoise, and green, with small square mirror fragments scattered like scattered light throughout the upper-left area. A softly rendered, dreamlike face emerges from the lower-left of the composition amid the teal tones, partially obscured by the surrounding texture and color. Warm gold, pink, and patterned fabric-like sections fill the lower right. The painting's surface has a visibly coarse, layered texture from mixed materials (fabric, mirror, acrylic, paper) rather than a flat printed look.
