## Hook
Everything in this one is moving — except the one thing in the middle. 🌿

## Caption body
"The Center Holds" — 10x10 inches, mixed media.

Spirals, leaves, little hand-drawn marks, all pulling the eye outward. And right in the center, one bloom just stays put. Rooted. Alive.

Small canvas. I like that it didn't need to be big to hold its own.

Psalm 1:3 keeps coming to mind when I look at it — planted by streams of water, bearing fruit in season, leaves that don't wither "in all that he does." Not because the weather around it is calm. Because the roots reach somewhere deeper than the weather does.
