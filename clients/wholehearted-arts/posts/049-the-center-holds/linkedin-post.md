## Hook
Everything in this painting is moving — spirals, leaves, little textured marks circling outward. And right in the middle, one bloom just... holds still.

That's the whole piece, honestly.

## Body
I finished this one at 10x10 inches. Small on purpose.

"The Center Holds" is mixed media — layered color, a lot of fine hand-drawn detail, a radiant floral center set against this luminous yellow-green ground. Everything around the bloom is in motion. Spirals, leaves, dots, texture pulling the eye outward. But the center itself stays rooted. Alive. Steady.

Looking at it now, it brings Psalm 1:3 to mind — the image of someone "planted by streams of water," bearing fruit in season, leaves that don't wither "in all that he does." Not because nothing is moving around that tree. Because the roots go somewhere deeper than the weather.

I don't think steadiness has to be loud, or big, to be real. Sometimes it's a 10x10 canvas. Sometimes it's just the one thing in a chaotic season that didn't move.
