## Hook
"Her children arise and call her blessed."

I've been sitting with that line from Proverbs 31 while finishing a new piece — because it's not about being perfect. It's about what outlasts you.

## Body
The painting is called "Wisdom of Mothers." Mixed media — fabric, paper, acrylic, layered in red, rose, gold, earthy tones. The garments in it carry Scripture worked right into their texture and pattern, because that's how I think faith actually gets passed down: not announced, just worn.

Proverbs 31 gets read a lot of ways. Sometimes as a checklist nobody can fully meet. But the passage itself tells you where the weight actually goes — verse 30: "charm is deceptive, and beauty is fleeting; but a woman who fears the LORD is to be praised."

That's the whole painting, honestly. Not an ideal to perform. A strength that's quiet, that shows up in the showing-up — sacrifice, protection, staying — and outlives the moment it happened in.

I think about the women who did that for me. Whose strength I probably didn't notice until much later.
