## Hook
I finished a new piece this week, and it kept bringing one verse to mind: "Her children arise and call her blessed" (Proverbs 31:28).

## Body
The painting is called "Wisdom of Mothers." It's mixed media — fabric, paper, acrylic — layered in red, rose, gold, and earthy tones, with Scripture worked right into the texture of the garments themselves. Not painted on top. Part of the fabric.

I wanted it to feel like that verse: strength that isn't loud about itself. The kind that shows up quietly, generation after generation, in the women who raised us and prayed over us long before we understood what they were doing.

Proverbs 31 gets read a lot of different ways — sometimes like an impossible checklist. But the passage tells you plainly, right near the end, where the real weight sits: "charm is deceptive, and beauty is fleeting; but a woman who fears the Lord is to be praised" (v.30). Not appearances. What lasts.

This one's a tribute — to the mothers, grandmothers, and women of faith who shaped us, whether or not they ever knew it.
