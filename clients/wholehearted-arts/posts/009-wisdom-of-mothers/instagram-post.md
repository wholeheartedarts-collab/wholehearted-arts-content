## Hook
"Her children arise and call her blessed." (Proverbs 31:28)

That's the line I kept coming back to while making this one.

## Caption body
Meet "Wisdom of Mothers" — mixed media, fabric and paper and acrylic, layered in red, rose, gold, earthy tones.

The garments carry Scripture worked right into their texture. Not printed on top. Part of the fabric itself.

Because that's how faith actually gets passed down, isn't it? Not announced. Just worn — quietly, generation after generation.

Proverbs 31 gets read a lot of ways, sometimes like a checklist nobody can fully meet. But the passage tells you where the weight really goes, right at the end: "a woman who fears the Lord is to be praised." Not charm. Not appearances. What lasts.

I think about the women who did that for me.
