## Hook
There's a painting of mine that's literally split in two — and it took me a while to realize that's exactly the point.

## Body
Above the line: a crowd stained red, carrying the weight of sin and emotional turmoil. Below it: figures in white, gathered around a well of living water, glowing with purity and renewal. Vibrant blues run through the lower half like hope pushing up against all that heaviness.

I named it "Cleansing Waters - Divided by Grace," because that's what the whole piece is doing — not rushing past the hard part to get to the beautiful part, but showing both, with a well right in the middle where one turns into the other.

It reminds me of Jesus at the well in John 4, telling the woman there that the water he gives "will become in him a spring of water welling up to eternal life." Or Isaiah's simpler version: "With joy you will draw water from the wells of salvation." Different words, same picture — a well as the place everything changes.
