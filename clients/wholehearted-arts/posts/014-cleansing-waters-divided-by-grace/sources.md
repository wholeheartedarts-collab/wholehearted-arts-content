## Note on scope of the scripture connection

Neither verse above is cited by number in the painting's own catalog
description — "well of living water" is Agata's own phrase. These verses
are documented here as *verified, resonant scripture that shares the same
image*, to be used in the post as an echo/association, not as a claim
about what the painting literally depicts or what Agata specifically
intended. See research.md's "Risks" section.
