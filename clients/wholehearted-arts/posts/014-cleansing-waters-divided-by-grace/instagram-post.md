## Hook
Above: a crowd stained red with the weight of sin. Below: figures in white at a well of living water.

## Caption body
Two realms in one piece — heaviness above, renewal below, and a well of living water as the place where one becomes the other.

"Everyone who drinks of the water I will give him will never be thirsty again... a spring of water welling up to eternal life." — John 4:14

That's the verse this piece keeps bringing to mind. Not because it's painted on the canvas — but because it's the same picture: burden, and then a well that changes everything.

"Cleansing Waters - Divided by Grace" — an original Atelier piece, 18 x 24.
