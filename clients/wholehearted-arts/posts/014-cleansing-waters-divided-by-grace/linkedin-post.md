## Hook
This painting is split in two. Above: a crowd stained red, carrying the weight of sin. Below: figures in white, gathered at a well of living water.

The turning point of the whole piece is that well.

## Body
I called it "Cleansing Waters - Divided by Grace," because that's exactly what the composition is doing — not blending the heaviness and the hope into one, but holding them side by side, with a well of living water as the hinge between them.

It's an image I keep coming back to in scripture, too. Jesus tells the woman at the well in John 4 that the water he gives "will become in him a spring of water welling up to eternal life" (John 4:14). Isaiah puts it even simpler: "With joy you will draw water from the wells of salvation" (Isaiah 12:3).

That's the whole painting, really. Not skipping past the burden to get to the beauty — showing both, with the water in between as the place where one becomes the other.
