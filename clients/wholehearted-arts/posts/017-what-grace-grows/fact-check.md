## Revision made during this pass

process/timeline claims presented as real events — e.g. "I've been
sitting with that verse for weeks," "I titled it before I fully
understood it," "I finished a new painting and only understood the title
after it was done," "while I was working on it, I kept thinking about a
verse." None of these are confirmed by any source; no writing samples or
statements from Agata about this specific piece's process exist yet.

personal anecdote), all three drafts were revised to present-tense
reflection on the *finished* piece ("looking at it now, I keep coming
back to...") rather than asserted past events. This preserves first-person
voice without inventing a specific unconfirmed backstory.
