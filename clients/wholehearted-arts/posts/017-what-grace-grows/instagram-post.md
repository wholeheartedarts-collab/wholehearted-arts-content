## Hook
Grace doesn't force itself into bloom. It just grows. 🌸

## Caption body
This one's called "What Grace Grows."

Pink, lavender, blue, green, gold — layered on, one bloom opening into the next until the whole canvas felt alive.

Looking at it now, I think of the flowers in Matthew 6: they don't toil, they don't spin, and yet not even Solomon in all his glory was dressed like one of them. No striving. Just growth, given.

That's the feeling this piece holds for me. Bright and peaceful at the same time. Expressive, but never forced.

Acrylic and pastel, 36x25", original.
