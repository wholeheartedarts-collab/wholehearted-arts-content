## Hook
"Consider the lilies of the field, how they grow." Looking at this new piece now, that's the verse I keep coming back to.

## Body
It's called "What Grace Grows" — a big floral canvas, pink, lavender, blue, green, golden yellow, all layered on top of each other in acrylic and pastel. Blooms opening across the whole surface.

Matthew 6:28-29 says flowers don't strive: they neither toil nor spin, and yet even Solomon in all his glory wasn't dressed like one of them. No effort. No anxious grabbing. Just growth, given.

That's the feeling this canvas keeps for me — bright, but peaceful. Expressive, but not forced. Energy and gentleness in the same brushstroke, because that's what grace actually looks like when I sit still long enough to notice it.

Grace grows. Not because we make it, but because it's already growing, whether we're striving or not.
