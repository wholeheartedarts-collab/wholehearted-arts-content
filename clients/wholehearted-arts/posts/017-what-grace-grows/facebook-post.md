## Hook
I finished a new painting, and I keep coming back to what its title means.

## Body
It's called "What Grace Grows" — a big floral canvas in acrylic and pastel, 36x25 inches. Pink, lavender, blue, green, gold, all layered together into blooms opening across the whole surface.

Looking at it, I keep thinking about a verse from Matthew: "Consider the lilies of the field, how they grow; they neither toil nor spin, yet I tell you, even Solomon in all his glory was not arrayed like one of these."

Flowers don't earn their color. They're not up at 5am striving for it. They just grow, because that's what they were made to do.

That's what this piece feels like to me — something bright and full and completely unforced. Grace that grows on its own, if we let it.

It's an original piece, freshly finished — one of a kind, the way they all are.
