# Research: "Gathered Light" (Atelier Collection)

## Executive summary

"Gathered Light" is an original mixed-media portrait from Agata's Atelier
Collection — a face built from layered fragments of pattern, texture,
handwritten marks, and luminous color, emerging softly through the
collage. The catalog description frames it around a specific tension:
fragility and resilience, a face that "emerges softly through complexity,
memory, and movement," and the idea that "light still finds us in
unfinished places." That image — light finding what is unfinished, not
waiting for it to be finished first — lines up closely with a
well-known verse, John 1:5, about light shining into darkness that does
not overcome it. This gives the topic a clear, verifiable scripture
anchor without requiring any invented backstory.
