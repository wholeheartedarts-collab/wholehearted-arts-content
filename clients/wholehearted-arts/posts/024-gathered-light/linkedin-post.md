## Hook
"Light still finds us in unfinished places."

That line kept surfacing while I made "Gathered Light" — a portrait built in layers: paint, fabric, handwritten marks.

## Body
I didn't plan the face first. It emerged — slowly, through collage and line work and a fair amount of starting over. Fragility and resilience sitting right next to each other on the same canvas, neither one winning.

There's a verse I keep setting next to this piece: "The light shines in the darkness, and the darkness has not overcome it." (John 1:5, ESV)

Not light after the darkness gets fixed. Light *in* it. While it's still unfinished.

That's the tension I wanted "Gathered Light" to hold — not a tidy, resolved image, but a face still becoming. Something built to reward slow looking rather than a quick scroll past.
