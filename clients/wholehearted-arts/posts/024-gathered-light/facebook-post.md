## Hook
There's a line from this painting's own description that hasn't left me alone: "light still finds us in unfinished places."

## Body
"Gathered Light" is an original mixed-media portrait — paint, fabric, handwritten marks, layer over layer, until a face starts to emerge. Not tidy. Not finished. Just becoming.

While I was living with this piece, I kept coming back to John 1:5: "The light shines in the darkness, and the darkness has not overcome it." Not light after the dark gets sorted out. Light right in the middle of it, while things are still unfinished.

That's what I wanted this piece to hold onto.
