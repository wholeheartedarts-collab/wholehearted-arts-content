## Hook
"Light still finds us in unfinished places."

That line kept surfacing the whole time I was making this piece.

## Caption body
"Gathered Light" is built from fragments — paint, fabric, handwritten marks, layer over layer, until a face starts to emerge.

Not resolved. Not tidy. Still becoming.

There's a verse I keep setting next to it: "The light shines in the darkness, and the darkness has not overcome it." (John 1:5, ESV)

Not light after the dark gets fixed. Light in it — while it's still unfinished.
