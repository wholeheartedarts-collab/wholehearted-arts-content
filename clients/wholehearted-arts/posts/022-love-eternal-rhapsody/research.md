## Risks, counterarguments, or limitations

- The product description references "the enchanting fairy tale of
  Cinderella" as a stylistic comparison — used only as the artist's own
  descriptive language about visual mood, not as a factual or religious
  claim, so it needs no separate verification.
- Colossians 3 contains verses beyond 3:12-14 that address household
  roles (3:18-19) and could read as more contested outside a faith
  context. This package draws only on Colossians 3:12 and 3:14 — the
  "clothe yourselves with compassion... and over all these virtues put
  on love, which binds them all together in perfect unity" passage —
  which is squarely about general virtues and love, not about roles.
  This keeps the post safely inside the client's no-politics avoid-list
  and matches what the painting's own description actually emphasizes
  ("virtues that nurture a strong and loving relationship").

## Possible content angles

1. **The virtues that hold a marriage together** — lead with Colossians
   3:12/3:14 (compassion, kindness, humility, gentleness, patience, and
   love binding them together) as the "recipe" quietly written into the
   painting's fabric and color, framed as Agata's own prayer for
   marriages. (Best fit: art collectors / faith audience — LinkedIn +
   Instagram + Facebook.)
2. **A wedding-day painting as a blessing, not just a portrait** —
   angle on gifting/commissioning art as a meaningful wedding or
   anniversary present, since the piece literally has scripture-based
   blessings hidden in its layers. (Good for Facebook's broader
   community/gift-minded audience.)
3. **Process/materials angle** — the physical layering (fabric, paper,
   gold and silver flake) as a metaphor for how a marriage is built in
   layers over time. (Good visual/sensory angle for Instagram.)

Angle 1 is the primary angle used across all three platforms for this
run, since it's the most directly grounded in the artist's own stated
inspiration and the verified scripture, and fits the "marriages" pillar
most cleanly without inventing personal backstory.
