## Hook
There's scripture hidden in this painting.
You won't see it at first — but it's the whole point.

## Caption body
"Love Eternal Rhapsody" started with a first kiss.

Layers of fabric, paper, acrylic, gold and silver flake — built up like years, not moments.

Written quietly into all of it, the words of Colossians 3:

"Clothe yourselves with compassion, kindness, humility, gentleness and patience... And over all these virtues put on love, which binds them all together in perfect unity."

Not the wedding-day version of love. The everyday version. The one that's still choosing kindness on the hard Tuesdays.

That's what I wanted this piece to hold.
