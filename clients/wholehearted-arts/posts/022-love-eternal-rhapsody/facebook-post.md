## Hook
This painting has a Bible verse hidden inside it — and it's not about the wedding day at all.

## Body
"Love Eternal Rhapsody" shows a couple on their wedding day, painted almost like a fairy tale — a bride and groom dressed like royalty, layers of fabric, paper, acrylic, and gold and silver flake built up the way a real marriage gets built, over time.

Woven quietly into that layering are the words of Colossians 3:

"Clothe yourselves with compassion, kindness, humility, gentleness and patience... And over all these virtues put on love, which binds them all together in perfect unity." (Colossians 3:12, 14)

That's really what this piece is a prayer for. Not the one perfect day — the thousand ordinary days after it, where a marriage is actually built one small kindness at a time.

If you know a couple who could use a reminder of that — an anniversary coming up, a wedding to celebrate, or just a marriage that's been quietly doing the hard, faithful work — I made this one with them in mind.
