## Hook
There's a Bible verse hidden inside this wedding painting. You won't see it at first glance — but it's the reason the piece exists.

## Body
"Love Eternal Rhapsody" started with a first kiss. A man and woman, painted like a royal couple on their wedding day — layers of fabric, paper, acrylic, and gold and silver flake built up like the years of a real marriage.

Written quietly into that layering are the words of Colossians 3:

"Clothe yourselves with compassion, kindness, humility, gentleness and patience... And over all these virtues put on love, which binds them all together in perfect unity." (Colossians 3:12, 14)

I didn't want to paint a wedding day. I wanted to paint what actually holds a marriage together after it.

Compassion when you're tired. Kindness when you don't feel like it. Patience, over and over, for years. That's not the fairy tale part — it's the part that makes the fairy tale true.
