Painting: After the Rain, the Windows Kept Glowing (Studio Collection)

## Hook
It's only 4 inches by 4 inches, but there's a whole evening inside it — wet streets, tilted rooftops, a few windows that kept glowing after the rain finally let up.

## Body
I love what happens in a piece this small. Layered paint, bits of collage standing in for brick and roofline, little flickers of light caught in the puddles. You have to lean in close to actually see most of it.

That's sort of true of a hard season, too. The best part is easy to miss if you're only looking at the storm.

"Weeping may endure for a night, but joy cometh in the morning." — Psalm 30:5

I don't think that verse means the hard part disappears overnight. I think it means the light was never actually gone. It was just waiting for you to notice it again.

## CTA
If you've got a window in your life that's still glowing after a rough stretch, I'd love to hear about it in the comments.
