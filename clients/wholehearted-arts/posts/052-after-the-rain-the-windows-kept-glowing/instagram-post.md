Painting: After the Rain, the Windows Kept Glowing (Studio Collection)

## Hook
It's only 4 inches by 4 inches. Wet streets, tilted rooftops, and a few windows that kept glowing after the rain.

## Caption body
This one's small enough that you have to lean in close to really see it.

Layered paint. Bits of collage standing in for brick and roofline. A little light caught in the puddles.

"Weeping may endure for a night, but joy cometh in the morning." — Psalm 30:5

The hard part doesn't disappear overnight. But the light was never actually gone.
