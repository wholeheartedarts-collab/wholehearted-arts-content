## Hook

I made a small painting recently that keeps asking me a big question.

## Body

It's about 8 inches across, a circle, with a sunflower in the middle and words wrapped around the bloom — a version of Psalm 27:1: "The LORD is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear?"

That psalm is attributed to David, and scholars widely read it as something he wrote while he was actually in danger, not looking back on it safely from the other side. And even inside the same psalm, a little further on, he admits the fear was still there. He wasn't pretending to be fearless. He just kept choosing to trust anyway.

That's what I wanted this piece to hold onto. Not the version of faith where fear disappears — the version where you're honest about it, and answer it with something steadier.

I've started calling it a small altar for the home. Nothing dramatic. Just something small enough to live on a shelf, where you'll actually look at it.
