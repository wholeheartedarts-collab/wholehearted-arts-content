## Hook

"The Lord is my light and salvation." Whom shall I fear?

## Caption body

Eight inches round. A sunflower turning toward light. The words circling the bloom like something you'd read every morning without meaning to memorize it.

It's a version of Psalm 27:1 — a psalm David is widely believed to have written in real danger, not after it had passed. Even in it, he admits fear was still there. He just kept choosing trust anyway.

I've started calling this one a small altar for the home. Not a statement piece. A quiet one — the kind you set somewhere you'll actually see it.
