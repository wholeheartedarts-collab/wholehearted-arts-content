## Hook
"My soul glorifies the Lord... from now on all generations will call me blessed." That's Mary, in Luke 1 — and it's where this painting's title comes from.

## Body
"They Will Call Me Blessed" is built on Mary's own words, spoken right after the angel Gabriel's announcement, during her visit to her cousin Elizabeth. Scholars call it the Magnificat — from the Latin for "my soul magnifies."

What strikes me about that passage isn't pride. It's the opposite. Mary names herself as "the humble state of his servant" in the very same breath she says all generations will call her blessed. The blessing isn't something she claims. It's something she's met with.

There's a parallel I keep coming back to when I look at this piece — the support is custom-constructed from salvaged materials. The linen is stretched by hand over a recycled wood frame, with a heavy linen curtain worked into it. Nothing here started out as something precious. It became that.
