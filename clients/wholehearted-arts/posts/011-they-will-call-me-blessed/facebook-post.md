## Hook
"From now on, all generations will call me blessed." That line is where this painting's title comes from.

## Body
It's Mary's own line, from Luke 1 — the Magnificat, spoken right after the angel Gabriel's announcement, during her visit to her cousin Elizabeth.

What gets me about that passage is what comes right before it. In the very same breath, Mary calls herself "the humble state of his servant." The blessing isn't something she reaches for. It's something she's met with.

There's a parallel I keep coming back to with "They Will Call Me Blessed" — the support is custom-built from salvaged materials. The linen is stretched by hand over a recycled wood frame, with a heavy linen curtain worked right into it. None of it started out precious. It became that.
