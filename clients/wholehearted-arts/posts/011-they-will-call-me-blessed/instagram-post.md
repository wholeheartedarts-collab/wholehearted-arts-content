## Hook
"From now on, all generations will call me blessed." — Mary's own words, and where this painting's title comes from.

## Caption body
"They Will Call Me Blessed" is built on the Magnificat — Mary's song of praise in Luke 1, spoken right after she learns she'll carry Jesus.

The part that gets me every time: she calls herself "the humble state of his servant" in the very same breath she says all generations will call her blessed. The blessing isn't something she claims. It's something she's met with.

The piece itself is made the same way. Salvaged materials, custom-built support, linen stretched by hand over a recycled wood frame. Nothing started out precious. It became that.
