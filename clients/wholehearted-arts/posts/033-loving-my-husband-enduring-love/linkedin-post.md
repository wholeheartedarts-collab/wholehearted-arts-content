## Post

The strongest man I know has never once needed to prove it.

I painted "Enduring Love; Strength through Trials" as an old couple folded into each other, with a city rising and falling behind them. A place torn down and rebuilt more times than anyone can count. Same as a long marriage.

I didn't paint us. But I know exactly why I painted it.

My husband loves me like nobody else does. Not loudly. In the ordinary ways nobody puts on a highlight reel: the things handled before I notice they needed handling, the steadiness when I'm not steady, the way he protects what matters to us without making a production of it. He loves being a strong man I can be proud of, and I want him to know, publicly, that I am.

"I found him whom my soul loves." (Song of Solomon 3:4)

We talk a lot about leadership on this platform. I've learned more about it from watching a man quietly carry his family than from any book: strength that serves, protection that doesn't control, consistency that nobody applauds and everybody depends on.

If you have a person like that, tell them today, out loud. And if you want, tell me one small thing they do that nobody else sees.
