## Caption

He's not loud about it. He just shows up. Every day.

I painted "Enduring Love; Strength through Trials" as an old couple wrapped around each other, a city rising and falling behind them. I didn't paint us. But I know exactly why I painted it.

My husband loves me like nobody else does. He cares in the small, unglamorous ways, the ones nobody posts about. He protects me without making a show of it. And he loves being a strong man I can be proud of. I am. Every single day.

"I found him whom my soul loves." (Song of Solomon 3:4)

Strength through trials isn't a slogan on our fridge. It's what happens when two people keep choosing each other after the easy part is over.

Tell me one small thing your person does that nobody else sees. 🤍
