## Caption body
"Never Stop Wonder" — 36x36, layers of florals, scribbles, bursts of light rising out of a dark ground.

Nothing about it was fully planned. That was the point. I wanted it to feel like thoughts and prayers and questions blooming all at once.

"Truly, I say to you, unless you turn and become like children, you will never enter the kingdom of heaven." — Matthew 18:3

Children aren't naive. They just haven't given up on asking "why" yet.

I don't want to either.
