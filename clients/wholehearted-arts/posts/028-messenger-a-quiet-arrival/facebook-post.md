## Hook

I want to tell you about the quietest painting I've made in a while.

## Body

It's called "Messenger" — one solitary bird, resting inside a layered,
textured landscape. No big gesture, no loud color explosion. Just a
quiet arrival.

The more I sat with it while I was painting, the more I kept thinking
about how many of the biggest moments in the Bible show up exactly like
that.

Noah's dove doesn't come back to the ark with a shout. She comes back
with one olive leaf in her beak, and that's enough — Noah knows the
water is finally receding (Genesis 8:11).

At Jesus's baptism, the Spirit doesn't arrive like fire from the sky.
It comes down "like a dove" (Matthew 3:16).

And after the wind, the earthquake, and the fire had all passed, Elijah
still hadn't met God — until what the Bible describes as a low whisper,
the "still small voice" (1 Kings 19:12).

Small. Quiet. Easy to miss if you're only listening for something loud.

That's the feeling I wanted "Messenger" to hold onto.
