## Hook

Not every message arrives loud. This one's a single bird, and a whole
lot of quiet.

## Caption body

"Messenger" — one solitary bird, sitting inside a layered, textured
landscape I built up slowly, piece by piece.

While I was making it I kept coming back to how many of the biggest
moments in scripture aren't loud at all.

Noah's dove doesn't come back with a shout — just one olive leaf in her
beak, and that's enough to know the water is receding.

At Jesus's baptism, the Spirit doesn't come like fire from the sky. It
comes down "like a dove."

And after the wind, the earthquake, and the fire, Elijah finally meets
God in what's translated as a low whisper — the "still small voice."

Small. Quiet. Easy to miss if you're only watching for something loud.

That's what I wanted this piece to hold space for.
