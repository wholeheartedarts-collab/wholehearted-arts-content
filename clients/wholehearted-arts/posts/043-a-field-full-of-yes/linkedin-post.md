## Hook
This one's called "A Field Full of Yes."

Small canvas. Big claim for a meadow of little flowers to be making.

## Body
It's 16x12 — layered greens, soft sky tones, pink and red and yellow scattered through it, the kind of piece you have to stand close to before you notice how much is actually happening in the texture. Nothing in it shouts. It rewards looking twice.

Every time I read the title back, it puts me in mind of 2 Corinthians 1:20: "For all the promises of God find their Yes in him." Not "maybe." Not "we'll see." Yes.

A field full of small flowers, each one doing its own quiet thing, felt like the right shape for that — not one big dramatic answer, but a lot of small ones, all pointed the same direction.

## CTA
Is there a "yes" you're still waiting to see the shape of? I'd genuinely like to hear it in the comments.

This piece is part of the current Studio Collection — one original, 16x12, and only one in stock.
