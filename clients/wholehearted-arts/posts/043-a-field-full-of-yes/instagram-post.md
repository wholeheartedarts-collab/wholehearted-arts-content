## Hook
A Field Full of Yes 🌾

Small canvas, big title.

## Caption body
16x12. Layered greens, soft sky, pops of pink and yellow — the kind of piece that asks you to stand close before it shows you everything in the texture.

I keep coming back to the title. It puts me in mind of 2 Corinthians 1:20 — "all the promises of God find their Yes in him." Not maybe. Yes.

A field full of small flowers, each one quietly doing its own thing, felt like the right shape for that. Not one loud answer. A lot of small ones, pointed the same way.
