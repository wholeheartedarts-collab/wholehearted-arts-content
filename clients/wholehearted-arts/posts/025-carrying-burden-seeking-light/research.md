## Executive summary

"Carrying Burden, Seeking Light" depicts two figures merged in an
embrace — one seeming to carry the other — rendered in cool blues that
speak to grief and vulnerability, set against warm rust, copper, rose,
and gold marks that read as light breaking through hardship. The
catalog description frames it explicitly around endurance, faith, and
the possibility of healing, and around a definition of love as
"carrying, holding, staying, and still searching for the light
together." That framing lines up closely, in ordinary usage rather than
as a doctrinal claim, with Galatians 6:2's instruction to carry one
another's burdens — a natural, low-risk editorial pairing for the piece
rather than a stretch.

## Key points (from the catalog/product description — the only source
for facts about this specific piece)

- Two figures appear bound together in an intimate embrace, blue forms
  merging into one another — described as holding grief, memory, love,
  and protection "in the same body."
