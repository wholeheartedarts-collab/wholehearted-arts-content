## Hook
There's a line I keep coming back to about this piece: love isn't always light. Sometimes it's carrying, holding, staying — and still searching for the light together.

## Body
"Carrying Burden, Seeking Light" is the newest piece from my Studio Collection — two figures bound together in an embrace, their blue forms nearly merging into one another.

That blue is carrying something real: grief, memory, protection, the weight we hold for people we love. Around the figures, warm rust, copper, rose, and small marks of gold rise up too — light breaking through the hardship, without erasing it.

There's a verse I keep landing on next to this one, Galatians 6:2: "Carry each other's burdens, and in this way you will fulfill the law of Christ." I didn't paint it onto the canvas — it's just where my mind goes when I sit with this piece. Some of the most faithful love doesn't look like ease. It looks like staying.
