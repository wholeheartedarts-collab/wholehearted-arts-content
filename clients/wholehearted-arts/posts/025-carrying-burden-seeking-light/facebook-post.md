## Hook
This one's called "Carrying Burden, Seeking Light," and it's stayed on my mind since finishing it.

## Body
Two figures, wrapped together in an embrace, painted almost entirely in blue — the color felt right for grief, memory, the weight we carry for the people we love. But look closely and warm rust, copper, rose, and small touches of gold are breaking through that blue too. Light, without erasing the hard part.

There's a verse that keeps coming to mind next to this piece, Galatians 6:2: "Carry each other's burdens, and in this way you will fulfill the law of Christ." I didn't paint the words in — it's just where this piece keeps taking me. Some of the most faithful love isn't the easy kind. It's the kind that stays.
