## Caption body
"Quiet Abundance" — hydrangeas, loosely gathered in a terracotta vessel. Warm gold, soft lavender, violet, blush, sky blue.

Hydrangeas don't have one fixed meaning. In Japanese flower tradition they're often tied to gratitude. Other traditions read them as abundance.

I like that it stays open.

Lately I've been sitting with the idea that quiet isn't wasted time — just a different kind of full.

This piece is small on purpose. Something to actually sit with, not just look at.
