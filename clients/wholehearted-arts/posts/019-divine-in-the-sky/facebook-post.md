## Hook
I don't think you need a special moment to see God — just an ordinary sky.

## Body
This piece is called "Divine in the Sky." It's mixed media — fabric, acrylic, alcohol ink, and gold leaf — and getting those materials to sit together peacefully took some patience.

Here's the detail I love most: Romans 1:20 is literally written into the horizon line of the painting. Not just the idea behind it — the actual words. That verse says God's power and nature have been "clearly perceived... in the things that have been made," ever since creation began.

I think about that on regular evenings, not dramatic ones. Sitting in traffic. Walking to the mailbox. Psalm 19 says the heavens simply "declare the glory of God" — no announcement needed, no perfect setting required.

If you've had a sky like that lately, I'd love to hear about it.

## CTA
Tell me about the last sky that made you stop, even for a second.
