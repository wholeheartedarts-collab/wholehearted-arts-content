## Hook
Proof doesn't always come from a sermon. Sometimes it's just the sky.

## Caption body
This one's called "Divine in the Sky" — fabric, acrylic, alcohol ink, and gold leaf, layered until they finally agreed to work together.

Look closely at the horizon line. That's Romans 1:20, written right into the piece: God's power and nature "clearly perceived... in the things that have been made."

Psalm 19 says it plainer — "the heavens declare the glory of God." Not on a mountaintop. Not on a special day. Just the sky, doing what it does.
