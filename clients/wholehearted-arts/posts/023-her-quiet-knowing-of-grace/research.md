## Executive summary

"Her Quiet Knowing of Grace" is a gentle mixed-media portrait on paper —
a woman's quiet gaze that Agata's own product copy describes as "tender
and strong," speaking of "inner blessing — the kind that does not need
to announce itself, but rests deeply within." That description maps
closely onto 1 Peter 3:4's well-known contrast between outward adornment
and "the unfading beauty of a gentle and quiet spirit" — a real,
verifiable scriptural anchor for the piece's theme of quiet, inward
strength rather than performed or announced beauty.

## Possible content angles

1. **Grace that doesn't perform** — the core angle: an "inner blessing"
   that "does not need to announce itself" versus a culture (and social
   media) that rewards announcing everything. Best fit: all three
   platforms.
2. **A gaze that is both tender and strong** — the specific visual
   detail (the quiet gaze) as an entry point into 1 Peter 3:4's "gentle
   and quiet spirit" being paired with "of great worth," not weakness.
   Best fit: Instagram/Facebook, more visual/personal framing.
3. **Portraits as a form of prayer** — painting a quiet inner state as a
   way of noticing/naming it, inviting the viewer to ask what their own
   "unannounced" strength looks like. Best fit: LinkedIn, slightly more
   reflective/process-oriented framing.

Recommended primary angle: **#1**, with #2's specific visual detail
(the gaze) woven in as the concrete hook, since "the concept alone"
risks reading as abstract without it.
