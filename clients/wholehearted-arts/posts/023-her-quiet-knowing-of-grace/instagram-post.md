## Hook
Some blessing doesn't announce itself. It just rests there, quietly.

## Caption body
That's what I kept thinking about while painting "Her Quiet Knowing of
Grace" — a portrait built in soft layers and expressive marks, with a
gaze that feels tender and strong at the same time.

"The unfading beauty of a gentle and quiet spirit... of great worth in
God's sight." (1 Peter 3:4)

Not weak. Not performing. Just real, and resting deep inside.

I wanted to paint that — the kind of strength that doesn't need an
audience to be true.
