## Hook
I finished a new portrait, and I keep coming back to one thing about it.

## Body
It's called "Her Quiet Knowing of Grace" — a mixed-media piece on paper,
built up in soft layers, with a gaze that somehow reads as both tender
and strong at once.

While I was working on it, I kept thinking about a line from 1 Peter:
"the unfading beauty of a gentle and quiet spirit... of great worth in
God's sight" (1 Peter 3:4). Not weakness. Not performance. Just a kind
of strength that doesn't need to be announced to be real.

I think we're all a little trained to think a blessing only counts if
someone notices it. This piece is my way of pushing back on that —
some of the deepest grace in a person is the kind that just quietly
stays.
