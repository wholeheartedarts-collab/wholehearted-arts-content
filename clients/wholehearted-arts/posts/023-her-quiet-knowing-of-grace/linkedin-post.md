## Hook
Most of the "strength" I see praised online is loud. This portrait is
about the kind that isn't.

## Body
I finished a new piece from the Studio Collection called "Her Quiet
Knowing of Grace." It's a mixed-media portrait on paper — soft layers,
expressive marks, and a gaze I kept coming back to while I was painting
it, because it reads as both tender and strong at the same time.

The idea behind it is simple, but it took me a while to actually paint
it honestly: some blessing doesn't announce itself. It just rests,
quietly, inside a person — visible in how they hold themselves, not in
anything they say about it.

There's a verse in 1 Peter that's stayed with me while working on this
one: "the unfading beauty of a gentle and quiet spirit, which is of
great worth in God's sight" (1 Peter 3:4, NIV). Not weak. Not passive.
Just not performing for anyone.

I think a lot about that distinction lately. Not everything worth
having needs to be loud to be real.
