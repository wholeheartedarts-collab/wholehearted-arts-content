## Hook
There's a version of protecting someone that comes from fear, and a version that comes from trust. My newest piece is about the second kind.

## Body
"Guardian of Innocence" is a 20 x 20 inch mixed media original, my newest addition to the Atelier Collection.

A mother rests with her child, eyes closed — not from exhaustion, but trust. Around them, fragments of color and texture come together the way I think a life actually gets shaped: through breaking and beauty both. Nothing wasted. Everything held.

What I keep coming back to with this one: protection without fear. Love that doesn't grip or control — it covers. It's guardianship that's honest about its own limits, and chooses to love fully anyway.
