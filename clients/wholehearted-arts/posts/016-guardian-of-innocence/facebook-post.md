## Hook
I want to tell you about the newest piece in my Atelier Collection: "Guardian of Innocence."

## Body
It's a 20 x 20 inch mixed media original — a mother resting with her child, eyes closed, not from exhaustion, but trust. Around them: fragments of color and texture, layered together the way a life actually gets shaped — through breaking and beauty both. Nothing wasted. Everything held.

What this piece speaks to, more than anything: protection without fear. Love that doesn't grip or control, but covers — the kind of guardianship that's honest about its own limits and chooses to love fully anyway.
