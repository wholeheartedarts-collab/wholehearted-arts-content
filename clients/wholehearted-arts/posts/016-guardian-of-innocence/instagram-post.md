## Caption body
"Guardian of Innocence" — 20x20", mixed media. My newest piece in the Atelier Collection.

A mother and child, held inside layers of color and torn texture — a life pieced together through breaking and beauty both. Nothing wasted. Everything held.

What I love most about this one: it's protection without fear. Love that covers instead of controlling.
