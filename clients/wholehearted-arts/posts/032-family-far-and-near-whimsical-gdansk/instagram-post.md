Theme: Loving family far away and family here in America
Painting: Whimsical Gdańsk (Atelier Collection)
Image: images/instagram.jpg (photo)

## Caption

An ocean between my two families. My heart never got the memo.

This is "Whimsical Gdańsk," a tiny 6x6 inch city glowing after dusk. Crooked rooftops, golden windows, little flags, layers of fabric and paint and metallic accents. It's the Poland I carry around. Not a map. A feeling.

Loving family from far away is a particular kind of love. It's phone calls at strange hours. It's knowing the weather in a city you don't live in. It's missing birthdays and still showing up in every way you can.

And then there's my family here, in America. The people who became home when home was an ocean away. I don't love them less for having a second home. I love them with everything I learned about love the first time.

"If I rise on the wings of the dawn, if I settle on the far side of the sea, even there your hand will guide me, your hand will hold me fast." (Psalm 139:9-10)

Who do you love from far away? Tell me where they are. 🤍
