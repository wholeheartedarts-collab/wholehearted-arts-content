## Hook

There's a verse I keep coming back to in the studio: "The flowers appear
on the earth, the time of singing has come." Winter doesn't announce when
it's ending. It just starts, quietly, to bloom.

## Body

My newest piece is named after that line — Song of Solomon 2:12.

I built it on plywood, layered with red poppies, fragments of music,
patterned papers, and the verse itself written by hand into the surface.
It leans on my Polish heritage, and on something I think about a lot as
both an artist and a person of faith: joy rarely arrives with a warning.

Most of the time, it just starts showing up in small ways — before we've
even noticed the season changed.

I named it "The Time of Singing Has Come" because that's exactly what the
verse is describing: not the end of winter announced, but the quiet
evidence that it already ended. Flowers first. Song after.

If you've ever felt a long quiet season start to lift — slowly, without
fanfare — this piece is for you.

## CTA

I'd love to hear it: what did your last "time of singing" look like, and
how did you know it had actually started?
