## Hook

Winter doesn't announce when it's ending. It just starts, quietly, to
bloom.

## Body

That's the idea behind my newest piece, "The Time of Singing Has Come" —
named after Song of Solomon 2:12: "The flowers appear on the earth, the
time of singing has come, and the voice of the turtledove is heard in
our land."

I built it on plywood — red poppies, fragments of music, patterned
papers, and the verse itself written by hand right into the surface. It
leans on my Polish heritage, and on something I keep noticing in my own
life: joy rarely arrives with a warning. It just starts showing up in
small ways, before we've even noticed the season changed.

Flowers first. Song after.

If you've ever felt a long quiet season start to lift — slowly, without
fanfare — I made this one for you.

You can see it here: https://www.wholeheartedarts.com/studio-collection-agata-christian-art/p/vxrrfngmvilbpyx58epf9qvlkrzbyx
