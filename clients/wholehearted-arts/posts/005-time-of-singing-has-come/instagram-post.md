## Caption body

This one's called The Time of Singing Has Come — Song of Solomon 2:12.

Poppies. Fragments of music. Patterned paper. The verse itself, written
by hand into the surface, on plywood.

It leans on my Polish heritage, and on a quiet thing I keep noticing:
joy doesn't usually arrive with a warning. It just starts. Flowers
first. Song after.

If you've ever felt a long quiet season start to lift before you could
name it — this one's for you.
