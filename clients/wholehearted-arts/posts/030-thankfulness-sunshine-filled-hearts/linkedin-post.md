## Post

Gratitude is not a mood. It's a way of looking.

The sentence I keep coming back to this month: thankfulness doesn't change what I have. It changes what I see. Same studio, same list, same unfinished canvases. Different eyes.

Paul puts it as an instruction, not a feeling: "Give thanks in all circumstances; for this is God's will for you in Christ Jesus." (1 Thessalonians 5:18) In all circumstances. Not for all of them. In them. That's a much more doable ask when the week is hard.

Behind these words is a blurred detail from my sunflower pair, "Sunshine Filled Hearts." I chose it on purpose. Sunflowers turn toward light without being told to. On my best days, so do I. On the other days, I write it down.

A practice worth trying this fall: before you open email, name three specific things. Not "my family," but the actual thing someone in your family did yesterday. Specific gratitude is harder to fake and harder to forget.

What's one specific thing you're grateful for this week? I'd love to read it.
