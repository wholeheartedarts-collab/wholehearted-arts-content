## Caption

Thankfulness doesn't change what I have. It changes what I see.

I'm not writing that because the holidays are coming. I'm writing it because I need it on an ordinary weekday, when the list is long and nothing is wrong, exactly, but nothing feels like enough either.

"Give thanks in all circumstances; for this is God's will for you in Christ Jesus." (1 Thessalonians 5:18)

In all circumstances. Not for all of them. In them. That's a different instruction, and a doable one.

Behind these words is a blur of my sunflower pair, "Sunshine Filled Hearts." Sunflowers turn toward light without being told to. I'm trying to learn the same move.

One thing you're thankful for today. Small counts. Tell me in the comments. 🌻
