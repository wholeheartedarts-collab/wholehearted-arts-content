## Note on Psalm 46:10 (not used as a source for this post)

Psalm 46:10's "Be still, and know that I am God" is the more famous verse
with similar language to this painting's title. This post does NOT quote or
attribute that verse's exact wording to Psalm 37 — see research.md's "Risks"
section. It is listed here only to document that the distinction was
checked, not because it's a source used in the post.
