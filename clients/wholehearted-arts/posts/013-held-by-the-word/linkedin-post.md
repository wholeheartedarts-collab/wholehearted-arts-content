## Hook
Psalm 37 doesn't tell you to fix your circumstances. It tells you to be still.

That's a hard thing to build a painting around — but it's the whole heart of my newest piece.

## Body
"Be still before the LORD and wait patiently for him; fret not yourself over the one who prospers in his way, over the man who carries out evil devices." (Psalm 37:7)

That's not stillness as relief. It's stillness as discipline — the work of not fretting while you wait, while everyone else's timeline looks faster than yours.

I made "Held by the Word" out of that discipline. It's a small piece — 10 x 20 inches, fabric and acrylic — with Psalm 37 worked into the background, underneath the color and texture you see first. Not decoration. Structure.

Because that's what I actually believe scripture does. It doesn't just get read and set aside. It becomes the background of how you live — how you endure, how you hope, how you stay steady when nothing around you is moving as fast as you'd like.
