## Hook
There's a verse tucked into Psalm 37 that most people don't know — because its more famous cousin, "Be still, and know that I am God," is actually from Psalm 46.

## Body
Psalm 37:7 says something a little different, and honestly, a little harder to live out: "Be still before the LORD and wait patiently for him; fret not yourself over the one who prospers in his way, over the man who carries out evil devices."

That's not the peaceful kind of stillness. That's the disciplined kind — the kind where you're waiting on God's timing while it looks like everyone else is getting ahead, and you have to choose, over and over, not to fret about it.

I made a small piece called "Held by the Word" out of that exact tension. It's 10 x 20 inches, fabric and acrylic, with Psalm 37 worked right into the background — underneath the layers of color and texture, not sitting on top as decoration.

That's how I actually think scripture works in a life. You don't just read it once. It becomes the background of everything — how you endure, how you hope, how you stay steady when the waiting is the hardest part.
