## Hook
"Be still before the LORD, and wait patiently for Him" — Psalm 37:7, and the whole reason this piece exists.

## Caption body
There's scripture worked into the background of this painting — literally underneath the color and texture you see first.

Not stillness as relief. Stillness as discipline — the hard part of waiting without fretting over everyone else's timeline.

I named this piece "Held by the Word" because that's what scripture becomes when you actually live with it. Not something you read once and set down. Something holding you while you wait.
