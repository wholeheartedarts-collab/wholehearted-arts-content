## Hook
Grace doesn't wait for the finished version of you.

## Caption body
"Where Grace Settles" — a 16x20 original, mixed media on wood. Soft blues, earthen reds, gold, green, a little blush.

It settles over the low places. The unfinished places.
The places still becoming.

Philippians 1:6 says He who began the good work in you will bring it to completion — the finishing is His job, not something you have to earn your way into first.

Still becoming is allowed. It might be the whole point.
