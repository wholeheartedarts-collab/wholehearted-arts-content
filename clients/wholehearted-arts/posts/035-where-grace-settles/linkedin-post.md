## Hook
I used to think grace was something you earned your way into once you'd gotten your act together. One of my paintings argues the opposite.

## Body
"Where Grace Settles" is a 16x20 original — soft blues, earthen reds, gold, green, a little blush. Mixed media on wood, catching light differently depending on where you stand.

The idea behind it is simple and it took me a long time to actually believe it: grace doesn't wait for the finished version of you. It settles over the low places. The unfinished places. The places still becoming.

That's not how I was taught to think about faith, honestly. I thought you got grace *after* you'd sorted yourself out. But Philippians 1:6 says it plainly — "he who began a good work in you will bring it to completion." The completing is His work, not a prerequisite you clear first.

If you're waiting to feel "resolved" before you let yourself be loved, led, or used — this piece is a quiet argument against that. Apparently unfinished is allowed. Apparently that's the whole point.

## CTA
