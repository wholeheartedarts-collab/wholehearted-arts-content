## Executive summary

"Where Grace Settles" is a 16x20 mixed-media original from Agata May'kowska's Studio Collection, built from soft blues, earthen reds, golds, greens, and gentle pinks. The painting's own listing describes grace as something that doesn't arrive by force but "settles over the low places, the unfinished places, the places still becoming" — a visual meditation on being in-progress rather than finished, and still held by grace in that state. This pairs naturally with Philippians 1:6's promise that God completes the work He starts, and with 2 Corinthians 12:9's teaching that grace meets us specifically in weakness and incompleteness, not after it's resolved.
