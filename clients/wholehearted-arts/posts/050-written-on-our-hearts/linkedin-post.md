## Hook
There's a line I painted fabric and acrylic around, and it still stops me every time I read it: God's law, written not on stone, but on a heart.

That's Jeremiah 31:33-34. It's the whole reason this small piece exists.

## Body
Most of what we're asked to live up to — rules, expectations, promises we've broken before — comes to us from the outside. Written down somewhere. Handed to us. Something to measure ourselves against, and usually fall short of.

Jeremiah describes something different: a covenant where the law isn't imposed from outside anymore. It's placed within. Written on the heart instead of on stone.

And the passage doesn't stop there. It ends with the line I think about the most:

"I will remember their sin no more."

Not overlook. Not tolerate. Remember no more.

This piece — "Prayerful Rose or Jeremiah" — is small on purpose. Ten inches by eight, fabric and acrylic, framed. I didn't want it grand. The promise inside it is big enough on its own.

## CTA
If you've ever felt like you were still trying to live up to something written on stone, I'd love to hear about it in the comments.
