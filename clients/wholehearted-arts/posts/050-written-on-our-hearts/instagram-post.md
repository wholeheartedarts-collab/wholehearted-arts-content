## Hook
Not stone. A heart.
That's the whole idea behind this piece.

## Caption body
I kept coming back to Jeremiah 31:33-34 while I made this one —

God's law, no longer written somewhere it can crack or wear down, but placed within. Written on a heart instead of a tablet.

And then the line that gets me every time:
"I will remember their sin no more."

Not overlooked. Remembered no more.

This piece is small — 10"x8", fabric and acrylic, framed. Felt right. The promise inside it didn't need to be loud.
