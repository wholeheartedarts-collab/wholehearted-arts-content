## Hook

I know, Lord, that You are right — even in the parts of this that
hurt.

That's not resignation. It's the first line of Psalm 119:75, and it's
what this small piece is built on.

## Body

"Finding Light in the Darkness # 2" is 8x10, framed to 11.5x14.5 —
fabric layered into acrylic, so the surface has real texture, not just
color. I like that it isn't smooth. Some seasons aren't.

The psalm it's built around doesn't skip to comfort. It goes in order:

First, naming it honestly — *I know that what You allow is right, even
the hard part.*

Then asking for comfort — not removal of the hard part, comfort inside
it.

Then asking for mercy, just to keep going — so I can live, and still
delight in what You've said.

That's the order I want to paint in, too. Not pretending the dark isn't
there. Not stopping at naming it, either.
