## 1. Executive summary

"Finding Light in the Darkness # 2" is a small (8"x10", framed to
11.5"x14.5") mixed-media fabric-and-acrylic piece from Agata's Studio
Collection, priced $80, 1 in stock. Per the product listing, the piece is

righteous judgment even in affliction, and asks for comfort and mercy so
he can keep living and delighting in God's law. The content angle is
hope found specifically *inside* hardship, not after it ends — fitting
squarely in the client's confirmed pillars of faith, emotion, healing,
and overcoming struggle while staying faithful to God.

## 6. Possible content angles

1. **Affliction-as-faithfulness** — the psalm's unusual claim that God's
   discipline, not just God's comfort, is itself an act of faithfulness;
   tie to the texture/fabric-and-acrylic mix as a visual metaphor for
   something rebuilt, layered, textured rather than smooth.
2. **The three-verse arc** — acknowledge → ask for comfort → ask for
   mercy to keep living; a short structural reflection on how lament
   moves toward hope rather than skipping to it.
3. **Small piece, present company** — using the $80, intimate 8x10 size
   as an invitation: a small, afford­able companion piece for someone
   walking through a hard season (gift angle, soft CTA).
4. **Studio Collection framing** — distinct from Atelier's larger studio
   pieces; Studio Collection pieces are more intimate/experimental in
   medium (fabric + acrylic here) — a chance to talk briefly about why
   she works in mixed media sometimes, without inventing specifics not
   in the listing.

Chosen primary angle for both platforms: **#1/#2 combined** — the psalm's
arc from honest affliction to asked-for comfort, grounded in the
piece's texture and scripture pairing. Angle #3 (gift/small-piece) kept
as a soft secondary note only if it fits naturally, not forced.
