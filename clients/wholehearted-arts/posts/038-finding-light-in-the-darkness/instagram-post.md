## Hook

I know, Lord, that You are right —
even in the part that hurts. 🕯️

## Caption body

That's the first line of Psalm 119:75, and it's what this piece is
built on.

"Finding Light in the Darkness # 2" — fabric layered right into the
acrylic, so the surface actually has texture. Not smooth. Some seasons
aren't.

The psalm doesn't rush to comfort. It goes in order:

Name it honestly.
Ask for comfort — inside it, not instead of it.
Ask for mercy, just to keep going.

That's the order I want to paint in too.
