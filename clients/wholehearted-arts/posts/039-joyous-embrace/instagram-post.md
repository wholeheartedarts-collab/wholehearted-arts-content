## Hook
This painting got damaged during my studio move.

Redoing it was the best thing that could've happened.

## Caption body
It never looked quite finished to me before — I just couldn't say why.

Then it got damaged in the move. I had to decide: let it go, or start over on it.

I started over.

Brighter colors. Bolder patterns. And it finally feels vibrantly alive — not patched up, genuinely different.

Mourning turned into dancing (Psalm 30 keeps coming to mind lately) — that's closer to what happened here than "repair."

Meet "Joyous Embrace." Mixed media on thick cardboard, 24"x19". Just one of this piece exists, and it's part of my Studio Collection.
