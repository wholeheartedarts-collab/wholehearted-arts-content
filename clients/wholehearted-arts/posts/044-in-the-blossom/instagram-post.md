## Hook

## Caption body

That's Calineczka for you — Poland's Thumbelina, born in the blossom of a flower. I was fascinated by her as a little girl.

I'm not sure how, but that same image came back to me years later, and it meant something different this time.

In Christianity, we talk about being reborn by the Spirit. But a seed can't do anything on its own — it needs ground that will let it grow. Jesus told a parable about that exact thing (Matthew 13): seed on a path, on rocky ground, among thorns, and on good soil. Same seed. Different ground. Only one grows into anything.

"In the Blossom" is that picture for me — a little girl's daydream that grew into something about grace, patience, and the kind of ground that lets us become who we're meant to be.
