## Agata's own description (source: painting-catalog.json, verbatim from the live product listing)

> What do you imagine? What happens when you sleep? As a child I was fascinated with the story of Calineczka (Polish for Thumbelina) - she was born in the blossom of a flower. I am not sure how I jumped from that into a phantasy about us being in the blossom of a flower when we sleep. It just sounded so magical to me and the child in me was just happy trusting that I am becoming prettier and better in my sleep.
>
> Later - I started seeing how it relates more to the renewing and rebirth. In Christianity we are ultimately reborn by the Spirit of God, but before that the seed must fall on the good soil. It will bear fruit in its right time. If the seed falls between rocks or weeds - it dies. But the good soil - there is a blossom and there is a fruit.

This is Agata's own voice already — a childhood memory (the Polish Calineczka/Thumbelina story) connected to a Christian reflection on rebirth and growth. No backstory is being invented beyond what she wrote; the post should draw directly from this.

## Executive summary

"In the Blossom" is a Studio Collection piece connecting a childhood fairy-tale memory (Calineczka — the Polish Thumbelina, born inside a flower) to a Christian reflection on spiritual rebirth, using the imagery of a seed that only becomes a blossom in good soil. The underlying scriptural reference is the Parable of the Sower (Matthew 13:1-9, 18-23), where seed falls on four kinds of ground and only the seed on good soil bears fruit.
