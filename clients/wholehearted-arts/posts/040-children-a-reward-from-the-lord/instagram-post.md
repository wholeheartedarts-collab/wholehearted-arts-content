## Hook
The prayer in this one isn't written below the painting. It's stitched right into it.

## Caption body
Fabric. Twine. White pen worked straight into the acrylic.

A father and mother, praying over the child they haven't met yet.

And around them, Psalm 127:

"Unless the LORD builds the house, those who build it labor in vain... Behold, children are a heritage from the LORD, the fruit of the womb a reward."

I love the first half of that psalm almost more than the line everyone quotes — all that early-rising, late-staying, anxious-toil striving, and none of it building anything on its own. Then it just settles. He gives rest. And a family isn't something you earn. It's given.

That's why I wanted real texture here instead of a flat illustration — things literally woven together, the way a family gets woven together. Slowly. By hand.
