## Hook
There's a difference between being seen and being looked at.

Most of us carry our hardest stuff in plain sight — we just assume no one's really looking.

## Body
I just finished a new piece for my "Heart of Healing" series. I called it "In Plain Sight."

Acrylic on canvas, 24x24, unframed, coarse finish — simple materials. The title is doing most of the work here, and I want to leave room for you to bring your own story to it rather than hand you mine.

Here's what I keep coming back to while I paint pieces like this one: so much of what we're carrying isn't actually hidden. It's visible. We just don't expect anyone to stop long enough to notice it, let alone tend to it.

Scripture says otherwise.

"He heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds." (Psalm 147:3)

"The LORD is near to the brokenhearted and saves the crushed in spirit." (Psalm 34:18)

Not distant. Not waiting for you to hide it better or explain it more clearly. Near — to exactly the broken part you're already not hiding very well.

That's the whole painting, honestly. Everything else is just canvas.
