# Brand Voice: WholeheartedArts (Agata May'kowska)

Status: **PROVISIONAL** — built from the client profile's stated voice
description plus LinkedIn platform best practice, not from real writing
samples. **Re-run this analysis after the first 3 published posts**,
using those posts and any human feedback as real samples.

## Core voice

Christian, artist, energetic, creative. Warm and personally expressive —
a real person writing, not a brand account or a "thought leader." Should
read like Agata is genuinely excited to share something, not performing
enthusiasm for an algorithm.

## Two-audience note

- **Art collectors** — voice can lean more sensory, personal, reflective;
  first-person process/feeling language is on-brand here.
- **Automation-buying businesses** — voice should still be warm and
  personal (never corporate), but leans slightly more toward concrete,
  practical value.
- This is an open question until real samples exist for both registers —
  don't flatten them into one identical tone.

## Do

- Write in first person, present tense where natural ("I love that
  part," "this piece feels...").
- Let faith show up naturally when it's genuinely part of the work (e.g.,
  scripture behind a painting) — not tacked on or preachy.
- Use concrete, sensory detail over abstraction (texture, material,
  light, color) — Agata's own site copy does this well: "the painting
  shifts as light moves across it... full of reflection, depth, and holy
  interruption."
- Short paragraphs, generous white space.
- Ask a genuine question or invite a real reaction rather than a hard
  sell.

## Avoid

- Generic AI/LinkedIn phrasing: "game-changing," "unlock," "in today's
  fast-paced world," "I'm thrilled to announce," "let's dive in."
- Fake intimacy or manufactured vulnerability.
- Unsupported superlatives ("the most powerful piece I've ever made")
  unless Agata actually says that.
- Sentimentality that tips into saccharine — genuine emotion, not
  performed emotion.
- Politics, even tangentially (client avoid-list).
- Claiming a specific personal story, result, or reaction from someone
  else unless Agata has confirmed it's real (see
  [[factual-verification]]).

## Example (illustrative, not from a real sample yet)

**Avoid:** "I'm beyond excited to share my latest piece — art has the
power to transform lives! 🎨✨"

**Prefer:** "There's a moment, right after someone stands in front of a
piece for a while, when their face changes. That's the part I make art
for."
